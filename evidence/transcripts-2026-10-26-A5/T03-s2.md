# Executive Workshop Proposal

**Client:** Tamarack Instruments

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client's AI portfolio is at the piloting stage:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that the Client holds no inventory of AI systems. The regulatory drivers named are the EU AI Act and GDPR. The Client intends to begin ISO/IEC 42001 work next year, although the start date is not fixed. The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system and an ISO 9001 quality management system.

The Sponsor's objective is stated directly: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." Three constraints shape the work:

- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits Now

**Maturity.** At the piloting stage, the Client has one system in use, one in trial and one in planning. This is the point at which ownership, risk appetite and oversight can be set before scale makes them harder to retrofit. A single day is enough to agree those foundations at leadership level and hand a clear mandate to the people who will build them.

**Timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. Holding the Workshop ahead of that date means the decision log and draft roadmap are available as inputs before it. The Sponsor's Q2 2027 objective then gives the roadmap a fixed anchor to sequence against.

**Integration.** AI governance at Tamarack Instruments will be positioned as an extension of the ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and the ISO 9001 QMS, not as a parallel system. The Profile does not state which registers, processes or records these systems hold. Specific attachment points are therefore `Unknown` and will be confirmed with the Client rather than assumed.

## 3. Workshop Structure

The day runs as four 90-minute blocks with breaks. It draws on the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen).

**Block 1: Alignment**
- Reviews the current AI posture across the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting.
- Treats the June 2026 audit finding on the missing AI inventory as the starting evidence.
- **Output:** a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2: Principles**
- Agrees the Client's governance operating principles, applying the three Academy principles to the Client's context:
  - **Slow AI** applies to the path from pilot to production for visual inspection.
  - **Informed Intent** applies to the planned sales forecasting before work begins.
  - **Final Liability rests with the Human** applies to every system, including customer-facing chatbot responses.
- **Output:** a set of principles ranked by the room.

**Block 3: Roadmap**
- Sequences governance work against the EU AI Act, GDPR and the ISO/IEC 42001 target, extending the existing ISMS and QMS.
- Treats works council consultation and the EU customer data requirement as sequencing constraints.
- Uses The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) to frame acceptance criteria for "governance in place."
- **Output:** a draft 12-month roadmap with owners.

**Block 4: Decisions**
- Executive decisions on governance ownership, risk appetite for AI, oversight cadence and budget mandate.
- Uses AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) as the reference for escalation beyond the executive layer.
- The block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and the twenty-minute closing exercise from the facilitation kit (https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md). This is the retention device for the three principles.

**Regulatory content.** Material on the EU AI Act and GDPR is orientation, not legal advice. The classification of each use case under the EU AI Act, and GDPR obligations for the chatbot and forecasting data, are flagged: confirm with external counsel.
