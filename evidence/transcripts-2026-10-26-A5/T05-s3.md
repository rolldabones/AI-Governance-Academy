## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has three AI use cases at different stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system and an ISO 9001 quality management system. The EU AI Act and GDPR are the stated regulatory drivers.

Three constraints shape the work:

- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff.
- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

The stated objective, in Dana Whitfield's words, is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

## 2. Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits Now

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The stated production target for visual inspection is Q2 2027. ISO/IEC 42001 work is planned to start next year, with no fixed start date.

Together these points favor building the foundation of an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) early and in a concentrated form. Doing so gives the Client the following:

- **A direct response to the audit finding.** The missing AI system inventory is built and classified during the week.
- **Time for adoption before Q2 2027.** Drafted governance artifacts can move through review and adoption by the Client's own governance bodies before the stated production target. Adoption timing remains a Client decision.
- **ISO/IEC 42001 readiness without front-loaded cost.** Artifacts are structured for future conformity work under ISO/IEC 42001. The Accelerator builds readiness. It does not deliver or promise certification.
- **Integration with existing systems.** Building on the ISMS and quality management system avoids a parallel governance structure that the Client would later need to reconcile.

The week is guided by three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice this means the Client's people make every classification and risk decision and own every artifact at close.
