# Practitioner Academy Proposal

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy
**Basis:** Client Profile Block v1.0 and discovery call notes of 6 October 2026

## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States, with about 1,100 staff. The Client's AI maturity is at the piloting stage. It has three AI use cases:

* **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
* **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
* **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. The Profile lists ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 as incumbent frameworks. Work toward ISO/IEC 42001 is expected to start next year, although the start date is not fixed.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that the Client has no inventory of AI systems. The Sponsor's stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

The Client has stated three constraints:

* Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff.

## Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

Tamarack Instruments already has one AI system in use and another in pilot. Governance therefore has to be built by the people who will operate it, while the systems are still taking shape.

The Practitioner Academy puts the proposed audience to work directly in the repositories:

* General Counsel
* CISO
* Head of Quality
* Head of Data
* German plant manager
* Two internal auditors

These roles span Legal, Information Security, Quality, Data and Internal Audit. That spread lets each draft artifact be challenged by the function that will later rely on it.

Starting an AI system inventory addresses the gap named in the June 2026 finding. Whether and how the finding is closed remains a matter for internal audit. The program is built on three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

## Running Worked Examples

Two use cases run through both days.

1. **AI visual inspection.** It is tied directly to the Sponsor's Q2 2027 objective. It also raises the works council constraint and has the most direct link to quality and production oversight.
2. **Customer service chatbot.** It is the only deployed system. It exercises the EU data residency constraint and GDPR considerations under live operating conditions.

AI sales forecasting is entered in the inventory and classified on Day 1. It is not carried further, because it is still at the planned stage.

## Confidentiality Approach

No customer data, production images or information about production staff enter the exercises. Participants work instead with sanitized analogs written during the sessions:

* synthetic support transcripts for the chatbot;
* a fictional defect catalogue and inspection log for visual inspection.

Participants work in Client-controlled copies of the repositories and do not place Client material in public locations.

Any workflow drafted for visual inspection that would touch production staff is marked draft pending works council consultation. The program does not put it into use. Whether that consultation has started is Unknown.

## Curriculum Spine

The program runs on two days, live and virtual, with four 90-minute working sessions each day.

* **Workbooks:** participants work in the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen).
* **Acceptance layer:** The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done).
* **Board layer:** AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
* **References:** module and pattern names below follow the curriculum spine. Exact file paths are confirmed in the pre-work packet.
