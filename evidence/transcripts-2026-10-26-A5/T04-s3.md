## 2. Why Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The Client has one system in production, one in pilot and one in planning. That combination lets practitioners build governance on live material instead of hypotheticals.

The visual inspection pilot has a stated production target of Q2 2027. That target gives the work a fixed horizon. The June 2026 audit finding also names a concrete first artifact: the inventory.

The Practitioner Academy places Legal, security, quality, data, operations and internal audit perspectives in the same working sessions. They build the first versions of the instruments together. The program follows three principles throughout: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

The Academy starts capability and artifacts. It does not by itself put governance in place, and it makes no certification or audit outcome promise, including for ISO/IEC 42001.
