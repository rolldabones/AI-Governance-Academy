## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at Piloting Maturity

At Piloting maturity, governance decisions are made under time pressure. One use case is live, one is approaching production and one is in planning. Each needs a different level of control.

A one-time assessment would describe the current position but would not support the decisions that follow it. Continuous fractional advisory provides senior guidance at each of those decision points. It does so without adding headcount and without displacing the Client's own accountable owners.

The engagement is guided by three principles:

* **Slow AI:** deliberate sequencing rather than rushed deployment.
* **Informed Intent:** decisions made with a clear understanding of risk and purpose.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** accountability stays with named people at the Client.

The work draws on three references:

* **GRC Workbook** (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance, risk and compliance structures.
* **Slow AI Kitchen** (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate, practical AI implementation.
* **AI Governance for Boards** (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) for board-layer reporting, where the Client chooses to use it.
