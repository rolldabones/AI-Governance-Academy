## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A consulting engagement would typically hand the Client a finished set of documents. A guided build works differently. The Client's own team builds the system each week, with workshop instruction and coaching alongside it.

This matters for Tamarack Instruments for three reasons:

1. **The audit finding stays with the Client.** It concerns a missing operating capability, not a missing document. Closing it credibly requires a team that can maintain the inventory after the program ends.
2. **ISO/IEC 42001 readiness depends on demonstrated operation.** It is shown through records generated over time. This program starts evidence collection in Week 8 so that operating records exist by Week 12.
3. **The program fits existing management systems.** The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and an ISO 9001 quality management system. A team that builds the AIMS itself can judge where it should connect to those systems and where it should stand alone.

The program is grounded in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. It works from two open frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Steering checkpoints draw on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
