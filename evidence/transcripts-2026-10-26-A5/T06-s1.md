## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Pure Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement typically delivers a finished document set to the Client. That approach can produce artifacts quickly. However, the knowledge of how they were built, and the habit of operating them, often leaves with the consultants.

The Guided AI GRC Build reverses this arrangement. Tamarack Instruments' own build team produces every artifact. Each week includes:

- one live virtual workshop to frame the work
- one coaching session to resolve the hard questions
- structured asynchronous implementation between sessions.

At Week 12 the Client holds a system it built, understands and can operate. This matters here for three reasons:

1. **The audit finding needs an owned process.** The finding concerns the absence of an inventory. An inventory maintained by people who did not build it tends to decay. An inventory the Client's own team built and owns is more likely to stay current.
2. **ISO/IEC 42001 readiness depends on internal ownership.** Readiness rests on demonstrated operation over time. The Client's team needs to run the processes before any certification work begins next year.
3. **Integration requires the people who know the incumbent systems.** Linking the AIMS to the existing ISMS and quality management system is best done by the people who operate those systems.

The program is grounded in three principles: **Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.** Concretely, every governed AI workflow built in the program names the human accountable for its outcomes.
