## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at the Piloting Stage

At the piloting stage, governance decisions are made alongside technical ones. A one-off assessment would describe the Client's position at a single moment. It would not stay with the Client while the visual inspection pilot moves toward production, while sales forecasting is scoped and while ISO/IEC 42001 work begins.

A fractional advisor provides continuity across these transitions. Governance artifacts mature in step with the systems they govern, and the executive team has a consistent point of reference when decisions arise between scheduled reviews.

The engagement is grounded in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, and Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice, this means pacing deployment to the readiness of governance, making each decision with documented intent, and keeping accountability with named people at Tamarack Instruments rather than with the advisor or the system.

The working frameworks are:
- **GRC Workbook** (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance, risk and compliance structure.
- **Slow AI Kitchen** (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate implementation practice.
- **AI Governance for Boards** (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) for executive and board-layer material where required.
