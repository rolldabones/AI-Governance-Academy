## 2. Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits Now

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. Your stated objective also sets a governance milestone ahead of Q2 2027. These two dates leave limited room for a long discovery phase followed by a separate drafting phase.

The Implementation Accelerator compresses that foundation work into five consecutive working days. Your team builds the core artifacts of an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) together, in live sessions, rather than reviewing them later in isolation.

The build serves your ISO/IEC 42001 target in a specific way. The artifacts are structured for future conformity work, so that later readiness work has a defined starting point. This is readiness work. It does not promise ISO/IEC 42001 certification or any audit result.

The build also responds directly to the June 2026 internal audit finding. Day 2 produces the AI system inventory the finding identified as absent.

Because the Client already runs ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 management systems, every artifact is designed to attach to those systems rather than stand alone. The specific registers, procedures or records within either system that the artifacts will attach to are `Unknown` at this stage.
