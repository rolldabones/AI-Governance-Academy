## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff and is headquartered in the Netherlands, with operations in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI portfolio is at three stages:

* **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.

The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function. ## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement would deliver a set of governance documents to Tamarack Instruments. The Guided AI GRC Build works differently. The Client's own team builds the Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) during the twelve weeks, with workshops, coaching and structured asynchronous work supporting each step.

At Week 12 the people who will run the system are the people who built it. They understand why each control exists and can operate, evidence and improve the system without depending on an outside party. This matters in three ways for Tamarack Instruments:

* **Audit continuity.** The internal audit finding on the AI inventory will be answered by a process the Client owns, not a document it received.
* **A head start on ISO/IEC 42001.** Planned ISO/IEC 42001 work will start from an operating base with evidence already accumulating.
* **Governance ahead of production.** Governance for the visual inspection pilot will be built by the roles that will carry it into production.

The program applies three principles throughout.

* **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
* **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

Delivery draws on two frameworks:

* GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
* Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Board-level reporting references AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
