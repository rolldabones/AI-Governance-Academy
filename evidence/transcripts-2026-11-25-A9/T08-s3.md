# One Workflow, Thirty Days

**Proposal to Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments (fictional)**

## Situation summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI use spans three stages. A customer service chatbot is deployed on the support portal. AI visual inspection is being piloted on the German production line. AI sales forecasting is planned for next year.

Dana Whitfield has stated the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded no inventory of AI systems. The Client's regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is targeted to start next year, and that start date is not fixed. A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

The Client has stated three constraints that this engagement will carry into its scope:

* A budget approved for this fiscal year.
* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## The workflow proposed and why it fits

We propose the AI visual inspection pilot on the German production line as the engagement's one workflow.

**Fit with the stated objective.** It is the workflow the stated objective names.

**Fit with AI_MATURITY (Piloting).** The engagement works on a workflow that is already in a pilot. It measures that workflow against a matched baseline under supervision before any production decision. It does not govern a hypothetical.

**Fit with the decision timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. How the Day 30 decision relates to that date is a question to confirm at intake. So is the start day that would place the thirty engagement days on Client working days.

**Final selection.** This proposal does not make the selection. The final selection is made in W02 against the benefit scale, from interview and work-sample evidence on four factors:

* volume
* active human effort per unit
* rework and queue burden
* consequence of better or faster output

The deployed chatbot and the planned forecasting work remain candidates for that scoring.

**Scope and the audit finding.** The engagement covers one workflow. It does not produce an inventory of AI systems. Whether its records should feed the Client's response to the June 2026 audit finding is a question to confirm at intake.

## Principles

The engagement applies Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human.

* **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
* **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.
