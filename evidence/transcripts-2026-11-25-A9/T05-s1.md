# Proposal: Implementation Accelerator for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Implementation Accelerator, AI Governance Academy
**Investment:** US$25,000 for five consecutive working days

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States, with about 1,100 staff.

The Client uses AI at three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

Several facts shape this proposal:

- **Audit finding:** In June 2026, an internal audit finding recorded no inventory of AI systems.
- **Regulation:** The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR.
- **Certification target:** ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year. The start date is not yet fixed.
- **Incumbent frameworks:** an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function.
- **Constraints:** Customer data must stay in the EU. The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff.

Dana Whitfield has stated the objective directly: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

## 2. Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The Implementation Accelerator is a fixed five-day program, so its scope, cost and outputs are known before that date. Scheduling the week is a question to confirm at intake.

**ISO/IEC 42001 readiness.** Since ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year, a structured foundation built beforehand gives that work a starting set of artifacts. The Accelerator structures every artifact for future conformity work. It establishes readiness. It does not promise ISO/IEC 42001 certification, audit results or any other assurance outcome.

**Building on existing systems.** The Accelerator attaches the AI Management System (AIMS) to the Client's existing management systems rather than creating a parallel structure. The ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, the ISO 9001 quality management system and the internal audit function are the intended attachment hosts. The specific registers, procedures and records within them that each AIMS artifact will connect to are `Unknown` and will be confirmed at intake.

**Methods and principles.** The week uses two published frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) serves as the board-layer reference for the Day 5 readout.

Three principles govern the work:

- **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

## 3. The Five-Day Build Spine, Tailored to Tamarack Instruments

The sequence follows two dependencies. The governance charter comes before policies, and the AI system inventory comes before the risk register.

**Day 1: Governance charter and roles.**
- The team drafts the charter's purpose, scope, roles, decision rights and meeting cadence.
- The program will propose a mapping between the charter and the ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and ISO 9001 governance structures. Attachment points are `Unknown` until confirmed.
- Executive checkpoint on the morning of Day 1.

**Day 2: AI system inventory and classification.**
- The team builds an inventory schema and populates it with the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting system as first entries.
- Fields capture purpose, lifecycle stage, data categories, data residency against the EU constraint, the named human owner and an EU AI Act orientation classification.
- The inventory responds to the June 2026 audit finding. Whether it closes that finding is for the internal audit function to determine.
- Any classification with legal effect carries the note: confirm with external counsel.

**Day 3: Risk and impact assessment plus risk register.**
- Working from the Day 2 inventory, the team runs impact assessments and builds a risk register.
- Seeded risk statements will address the three AI use cases and the June 2026 audit finding. Every seeded statement is labeled as a draft for Client validation.

**Day 4: Core policies and operating procedures.**
