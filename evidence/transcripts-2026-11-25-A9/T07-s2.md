## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. ## 2. Why Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Stage

At Piloting maturity, the governance questions that matter most arise while systems move between stages, not at a single point in time. Over the next twelve months, the Client has three systems at three different stages. It also has a production target for visual inspection and a certification program with an open start date.

A one-time assessment would capture this picture once. A fractional advisor stays with the decisions as they arise and keeps the work anchored in three principles:

* **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
* **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

The program works from two frameworks: the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). Executive and board-level material draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
