# Guided AI GRC Build: Proposal for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Guided AI GRC Build, AI Governance Academy
**Duration and price:** 12 weeks, US$60,000

Dana Whitfield,

This proposal sets out how the Guided AI GRC Build would give Tamarack Instruments a functioning minimum viable Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) in twelve weeks. It is built and retained by your own team.

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States.

The Client's AI portfolio sits at three stages:

* **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
* **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
* **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 is a certification target, with work targeted to start next year. The start date is not yet fixed.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded no inventory of AI systems.

Your stated objective is clear: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

Three constraints shape the build:

* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.
* Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement would hand Tamarack a finished governance framework. The Client's team would then have to learn to operate it after the consultants leave.

The Guided AI GRC Build reverses that sequence. Your build team produces every artifact itself, with weekly workshops, coaching and structured asynchronous work between sessions. By Week 12 the people who will run the AIMS have already written its charter, populated its inventory, assessed its risks and operated its workflows. The system belongs to Tamarack from the first week.

The build draws on three open frameworks:

* **GRC Workbook** (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) provides the governance, risk and compliance structure.
* **Slow AI Kitchen** (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) provides the method for governed AI workflows and human oversight.
* **AI Governance for Boards** (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) is the reference for steering and executive reporting.

Three principles run through the program:

* **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
* **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.
