# One Workflow, Thirty Days

**Proposal to Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments**

## Situation

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands, operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States and has about 1,100 staff. Its AI portfolio has three parts:

* a customer service chatbot deployed on the support portal
* AI visual inspection in pilot on the German production line
* AI sales forecasting planned for next year

The Client has stated its regulatory drivers as the EU AI Act and GDPR. An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. Work toward ISO/IEC 42001 is planned to start next year, with the start date not yet fixed. A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

Dana Whitfield has set the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

The Client has stated three constraints:

* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.
* Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

## The Proposed Workflow

We propose the AI visual inspection pilot on the German production line as the engagement's one workflow.

**Fit with maturity.** The Client's AI maturity is Piloting, and this use case is the one in pilot. A pilot can be carried through the twelve steps of the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) and measured against a matched baseline before any production commitment. That makes it a direct test of the stated objective.

**Fit with the decision timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. Whether Day 30 should fall before that date is a question to confirm at intake. The thirty elapsed engagement days are placed on Client working days.

**Final selection.** The final selection is made in W02 against the Workbook's benefit scale. The deployed chatbot and the planned forecasting work are also scored there. The four factors are:

* volume
* active human effort per unit
* rework and queue burden
* consequence of better or faster output

Each factor is scored from interview and work-sample evidence, not from this proposal.

**Questions to confirm at intake:**

* whether the visual inspection pilot touches production staff, and therefore when works council consultation must occur relative to the bounded rehearsal
* who will sign W01 as business owner and who acts as technical owner and receiving owner (each is `Unknown`)
* whether any engagement material would contain customer data, so the EU data constraint can be applied to evidence handling
