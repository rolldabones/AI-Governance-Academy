# Executive Workshop Proposal

**Client:** Tamarack Instruments (fictional)
**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** AI Governance Academy, Executive Workshop
**Format and price:** One day, live virtual, US$7,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States.

The Client's AI use spans three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded no inventory of AI systems.

The stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

**Regulatory drivers:** the EU AI Act and GDPR.

**Certification target:** work toward ISO/IEC 42001 is planned to start next year. The start date is not fixed.

**Existing frameworks:**

- an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system
- an ISO 9001 quality management system
- an internal audit function

**Stated constraints:**

- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.
- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

The Client's AI maturity is Piloting, with one system deployed, one in pilot and one planned. At this stage, decisions on ownership, risk appetite, oversight cadence and budget mandate shape every later piece of governance work. Those decisions belong to leadership and are best taken together.

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The stated objective sets Q2 2027 as the point by which governance should be in place for visual inspection.

One day gives leadership:

- a shared statement of intent
- agreed principles
- a sequenced roadmap
- a decision log, produced within a fixed window

The Workshop does not replace build work. It establishes the mandate and sequence that build work requires. The fee of US$7,500 sits within the budget approved for this fiscal year.

## 3. Four-Block Structure with Client-Specific Emphasis

The Workshop draws on two frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

**Block 1, Alignment (90 minutes).**
- **Focus:** the current AI posture across the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting, together with the June 2026 internal audit finding.
- **Emphasis:** what an AI inventory must contain, using the inventory domain of the GRC Workbook.
- **Output:** a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2, Principles (90 minutes).**
The room works through the three Academy principles.

- **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed. The three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

The room applies these principles to two cases:

- the visual inspection pilot ahead of production
- sales forecasting before it is built

The room then ranks the principles it adopts.

**Block 3, Roadmap (90 minutes).**
- **Sequencing:** governance work is sequenced against the EU AI Act, GDPR and the ISO/IEC 42001 target.
- **Integration:** AI governance is positioned as an extension of the ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system, the ISO 9001 quality management system and the internal audit function. Specific attachment points within these frameworks, such as registers, processes, controls or records, are `Unknown` and will be confirmed at intake.
- **Constraints:** EU data residency and works council consultation are treated as sequencing inputs.
- **Acceptance criteria:** roadmap items draw on The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done).
- **Regulatory content:** this is orientation, not legal advice. The applicability and classification of each use case under the EU AI Act and GDPR should be confirmed with external counsel.
- **Output:** a draft 12-month roadmap with owners.

**Block 4, Decisions (90 minutes).**
- **Decisions on record:** governance ownership, risk appetite for AI, oversight cadence and budget mandate.
- **Oversight reference:** oversight cadence is framed with reference to AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
- **Closing:** the block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and the twenty-minute closing exercise from the facilitation kit (https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md). This is the retention device for the three principles.
