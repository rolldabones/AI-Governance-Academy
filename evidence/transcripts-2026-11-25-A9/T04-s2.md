## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI portfolio has three systems at different stages:

* A customer service chatbot on the support portal is deployed.
* AI visual inspection on the German production line is in pilot.
* AI sales forecasting is planned for next year.

The Client's regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is planned to start next year, and the start date is not fixed. An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems.

Dana Whitfield has stated the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

The Client has stated these constraints:

* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.
* Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

## Why Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The Practitioner Academy builds working capability in the people who will run AI governance day to day. Participants do not study governance in the abstract. They begin producing the Client's own artifacts inside two open repositories they keep after the program ends.

Tamarack Instruments has one deployed system, one pilot and one planned system. That spread lets participants practice governing AI at three points in its life:

* **The deployed chatbot:** after the fact.
* **The visual inspection pilot:** before production.
* **Sales forecasting:** before any build begins.

Day 1 starts an AI system inventory. That inventory is the subject of the June 2026 internal audit finding. Whether and when the finding is closed remains a matter for the Client's internal audit function. This program does not promise any audit or certification outcome, including for ISO/IEC 42001.

The program is anchored in three principles:

* **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
* **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

## Running Worked Examples

All three AI use cases serve as worked examples across both days.

1. **AI visual inspection.** This system is the primary example. It ties directly to the Q2 2027 objective, and its pilot stage leaves room to shape governance before production.
2. **Customer service chatbot.** As the deployed system, it is the natural test of inventory, classification and evidence for something already live.
3. **AI sales forecasting.** As a planned system, it is used to practice Informed Intent authorization before any work begins.

## Confidentiality Approach

No real customer data, production data or personal data enters the sessions or the repositories. Every exercise uses a sanitized analog of each system: a structurally similar description with fictional inputs and outputs.

* **EU data constraint:** this approach respects the requirement that customer data stays in the EU.
* **Works council:** no exercise involves information about production staff. We will confirm at intake whether the German works council should be informed of the program itself.
