## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at the Piloting Stage

At the piloting stage, governance decisions recur rather than arrive once. The Client has one system already in use, one moving toward production and one in planning. Each needs authorization, ownership and evidence at a different point in the year. A single assessment would capture the position on one date and then age. A fractional advisor stays alongside the governance team as each system moves forward.

The program works from three principles:

* **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
* **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

The working frameworks are:

* the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
* the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Executive and board reporting draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
