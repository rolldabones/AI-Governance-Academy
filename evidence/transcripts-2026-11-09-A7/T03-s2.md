- **Stated objective:** "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." (Dana Whitfield)

  - The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

**Maturity.** The Client's AI maturity is Piloting. It has one deployed system, one pilot and one planned use case. A single day lets leadership set governance intent, principles and ownership while the portfolio is still small enough to examine case by case in one room.

**Timing.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The one-day format can be scheduled and delivered ahead of that date if the Client wishes. The stated objective names Q2 2027, so the workshop's draft 12-month roadmap will place that milestone inside its horizon.

**Budget.** The Executive Workshop is priced at US$7,500. The Client's stated budget approval is up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
