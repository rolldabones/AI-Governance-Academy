A pilot moving toward production, a deployed system and a planned system each call for different governance treatment. ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work then adds a management system layer over all three.

A one-time assessment would describe a single moment. A fractional advisor stays engaged as the visual inspection pilot approaches its stated production window, as ISO/IEC 42001 work begins and as sales forecasting moves from plan to design.

* Governance gates are set before production rather than after.
