## 2. Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits

The Implementation Accelerator builds the foundation of an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) in five working days. It produces a governance charter, an AI system inventory, a risk register, core policies and an evidence model. The Client owns all of these at close.

This format suits the Client's timing in three ways:

1. **It responds directly to the audit finding.** The June 2026 finding concerns the absence of an AI system inventory. The Accelerator builds that inventory on Day 2 and seeds it with the three use cases recorded in the Profile Block.
2. **It leaves time before the stated production milestone.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026, and the stated objective sets Q2 2027 for visual inspection going into production. Completing the foundation in one working week leaves the remaining time for adoption, validation and works council consultation, ahead of that milestone.
3. **It prepares for ISO/IEC 42001 without promising certification.** Because ISO/IEC 42001 is a certification target, every artifact is structured for future conformity work. The aim is readiness. The Accelerator does not promise certification, audit outcomes or SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2) results.
