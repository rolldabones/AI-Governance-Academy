At the Piloting stage, governance decisions are made while systems are still changing. The Profile Block shows one system deployed, one in pilot with a stated production objective in Q2 2027 and one planned for next year.

A single assessment would describe one moment in that sequence. A twelve-month fractional engagement stays alongside the Client as each system moves through its stage. It also keeps the ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work connected to the systems the Client is actually operating.

The program is built on three principles:

- **Slow AI:** decisions are paced to the evidence available.
- **Informed Intent:** each AI use has a documented purpose and owner.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human:** accountability for AI outcomes stays with named people at the Client.
