**Timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The one-day format can be scheduled and its outputs returned on a short cycle, subject to the date confirmed at intake.

The Workshop runs live and virtual for one day in four 90-minute blocks with breaks.

**Block 1, Alignment**
- The room reviews current AI posture using the three stated use cases and the June 2026 internal audit finding.
