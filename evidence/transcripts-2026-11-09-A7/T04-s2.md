## 2. Why Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The Practitioner Academy is a working program. Participants leave with governance artifacts started in the repositories they will keep using, not only with notes. Four Profile Block facts make it a fit at this point:

* **A dated target.** Your stated objective sets Q2 2027 as the point by which governance should be in place for visual inspection.
* **A named gap.** The June 2026 finding names the absence of an AI inventory, which is the first artifact the program starts.
* **Three use cases at three stages.** Deployed, piloting and planned systems give participants a full lifecycle to practise on.
* **Mixed audience roles.** General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and two internal auditors can build shared artifacts in the same sessions.

The program teaches three principles, carried through every exercise:

* **Slow AI**
* **Informed Intent**
* **Final Liability rests with the Human**
