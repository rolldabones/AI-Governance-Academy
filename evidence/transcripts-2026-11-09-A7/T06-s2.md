## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement typically hands over a finished set of documents. The Guided AI GRC Build works differently. Your team does the building, with weekly structure, coaching and review from us. At Week 12 Tamarack Instruments therefore holds a system its own people have built, understand and can operate. - **Continuity toward ISO/IEC 42001.** Readiness depends on people who can run the system after the program ends.
- **Integration with existing disciplines.** Your team can attach AI governance to existing ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 artifacts where they exist, rather than create parallel structures.
- **Continued ownership of AI decisions.** Accountability stays with named Tamarack Instruments roles.

The program operates on three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

We work from two open frameworks: the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). For steering checkpoints we draw on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
