## 2. Proposed Workflow and Fit

**Proposed workflow:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.

This candidate fits the Client for three reasons:

- **Objective.** It is the workflow named in the stated objective.
- **Maturity.** It matches the Client's AI_MATURITY of Piloting. A system already in pilot can be taken through a bounded rehearsal and a matched baseline. A system that is only planned cannot.

The other candidates are weaker fits at this point:

- **Sales forecasting** is planned for next year, so there is no current workflow to measure.

This is a proposal, not a selection. The final selection is made in W02 against the benefit scale, using four factors: volume, active human effort per unit, rework and queue burden and consequence of better or faster output.
