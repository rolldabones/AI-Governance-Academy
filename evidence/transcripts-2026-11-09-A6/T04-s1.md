# Practitioner Academy Proposal

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy
**Basis:** Client Profile Block v1.0 and discovery call notes of 6 October 2026

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. The Client is headquartered in the Netherlands, operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States and has about 1,100 staff. The Client's AI footprint is at the piloting stage:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The Client names the EU AI Act and GDPR as its regulatory drivers. It intends to start ISO/IEC 42001 work next year, though the start date is not fixed. An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The Sponsor's stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

Three constraints shape this proposal:

- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

The Client lists an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function as incumbent frameworks. This proposal does not assume any existing register, process, control or record within them. Where reuse is possible, the Client confirms it during the program.

## 2. Why Practitioner Capability Fits the Client Now

The audit finding and the Sponsor's Q2 2027 objective point to the same need. The Client needs people who can build and maintain governance artifacts themselves, starting with an AI system inventory.

The Practitioner Academy puts the people who will own that work in the same room for two days. Participants start the artifacts in the repositories they will keep using afterwards. The work proceeds under three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

The participant group named for this program is:

- General Counsel
- CISO
- Head of Quality
- Head of Data
- German plant manager
- Two internal auditors

This group spans legal, security, quality, data, operations and assurance. These are the perspectives the three use cases require.

## 3. Worked Examples and Tailoring

Two use cases run through both days as worked examples:

1. **AI visual inspection (piloting).** This is the system named in the Sponsor's objective. It also carries the works council constraint and an EU AI Act classification question that participants will work through. This proposal does not pre-judge that classification.
2. **Customer service chatbot (deployed).** This is the only system currently in use. It brings GDPR questions and the EU data residency constraint into the exercises.

Sales forecasting is planned rather than in use. It enters the inventory and classification exercise only, so the inventory covers all three known systems.

**Confidentiality.** All exercises use sanitized analogs:

- Synthetic support conversations stand in for customer data.
- Described inspection steps and defect categories stand in for line data and images.

No exercise collects data from or about production staff. Workflows that would affect production staff leave the program as drafts, flagged for works council consultation before any use. Whether delivering the program itself requires works council consultation is Unknown. The Client should confirm this.
