## 2. Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits Now

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. Your stated objective places governance ahead of a Q2 2027 production milestone for visual inspection. That leaves a narrow window, and a five-day intensive build fits it for three reasons.

**It answers the audit finding first.** The June 2026 finding concerns the absence of an AI system inventory. The Accelerator builds that inventory on Day 2, using the Client's three known use cases as first entries. Whether the inventory closes the finding is for the Client's internal audit function to determine.

**It prepares for ISO/IEC 42001 without waiting for a start date.** The ISO/IEC 42001 start date is not fixed. Every artifact is structured for future conformity work, so the Client begins that work with a foundation in place rather than a blank page. This is readiness work. It does not promise certification or any audit result.

The Accelerator designs each artifact to attach to these systems rather than stand alone.
