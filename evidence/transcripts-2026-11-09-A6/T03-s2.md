# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel (Sponsor)
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy
**Format:** One day, live virtual | **Price:** US$7,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has AI at three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The Client names the EU AI Act and GDPR as regulatory drivers. It also names ISO/IEC 42001 as a certification target, with work to start next year on a date not yet fixed.

The objective, in the Sponsor's words, is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

Three constraints shape the work:

- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits Now

The Client's AI maturity is Piloting. At this stage, the most valuable governance decisions are made before a pilot becomes a production system. They become harder to make afterwards.

The visual inspection pilot and the Q2 2027 objective give the leadership team a concrete case to govern against. The audit finding gives them a documented starting point.

A one-day session puts the following roles in one room to agree on intent, principles, sequence and ownership:

- General Counsel
- CISO
- Head of Quality
- Head of Data
- German plant manager
- Two internal auditors

Each role is listed in the Profile Block. Their decisions are recorded in a form that can be acted on.

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The Workshop date is to be agreed with the Client with that date in view.

## 3. Integration With Existing Frameworks

The Workshop positions AI governance as an extension of the Client's ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, ISO 9001 quality management system and internal audit function. It does not create a parallel structure.

The Profile Block names these frameworks but does not state which registers, processes or records they contain. The attachment points below are therefore recorded as follows until confirmed:

- **The Definition of Done** (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done): supports agreement on what "governance in place" means as acceptance criteria.

## 4. Four-Block Structure With Client-Specific Emphasis

**Block 1, Alignment (90 minutes)**
Participants map current AI posture across the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting, using the June 2026 audit finding as the anchor. The block produces a shared statement of governance intent. It includes a first working definition of what "governance in place" must mean before visual inspection enters production.

**Block 2, Principles (90 minutes)**
Participants agree governance operating principles, including the three Academy principles applied to the Client's context:

- **Slow AI** applies to the pace and gates of the move from pilot to production.
- **Informed Intent** applies to the purpose and boundaries of each use case, including the planned forecasting work before it starts.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human** applies to who answers for outcomes that each AI system informs.

The room ranks the agreed principles.

**Block 3, Roadmap (90 minutes)**
Participants sequence governance work against:

- the EU AI Act and GDPR
- the ISO/IEC 42001 target
- the incumbent ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 frameworks and the internal audit function

The constraints are built in as sequencing conditions:

- EU data residency for customer data.
- Works council consultation before anything touches production staff. Whether and when this applies to visual inspection is to be determined.
- The approved budget.

The block produces a draft 12-month roadmap with owners and a Q2 2027 milestone tied to the stated objective.

**Block 4, Decisions (90 minutes)**
Participants make and record decisions on:

- governance ownership
- risk appetite for AI
- oversight cadence
- budget mandate

The block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* ([facilitation kit](https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md)) and the kit's twenty-minute closing exercise. This serves as the retention device for the three principles.
