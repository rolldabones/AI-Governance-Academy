## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Stage

At Piloting maturity, governance decisions are being made for the first time. Each one sets a precedent for the systems that follow. The Client faces three of these decisions in close sequence:

- moving visual inspection from pilot to production
- bringing the deployed chatbot under formal governance
- preparing for sales forecasting before it is built

A one-time assessment would describe the current position but would not stay with the Client through these decisions. A continuous fractional model offers three things:

- **Senior judgment at each decision point**, without a permanent hire.
- **Sequenced work.** The inventory gap identified by internal audit is closed first, because later governance steps depend on it.
- **Room for ISO/IEC 42001 readiness to build gradually** alongside operational work, rather than as a late-stage project.

The engagement draws on two frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance, risk and compliance artifacts
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate, staged AI adoption
