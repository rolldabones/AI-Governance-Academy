# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**To:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments
**From:** AI Governance Academy
**Program:** Executive Workshop (one day, live virtual, US$7,500)

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States.

The Client's AI use cases sit at three different stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding from June 2026 records that there is no inventory of AI systems.

The Profile Block names these regulatory drivers: the EU AI Act and GDPR. It names ISO/IEC 42001 (https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) as a certification target, with work to start next year and the start date not fixed.

The Client's incumbent frameworks are:

- an ISO/IEC 27001 (https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) information security management system
- an ISO 9001 (https://www.iso.org/iso-9001-quality-management.html) quality management system
- an internal audit function

The stated objective, in your words: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

The stated constraints are:

- budget approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year
- customer data must stay in the EU
- the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

**Fit with AI maturity.** The Client's AI maturity is recorded as Piloting. At this stage the most useful step is to align leadership on intent, principles and ownership before practices form around individual systems. Building detailed controls can follow once that alignment exists. A single day gives the leadership group a shared starting point without committing the Client to a larger program before its direction is set.

**Fit with the decision timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. A one-day format can be scheduled and completed before that date if the Client chooses.

**Fit with the audit finding.** The June 2026 finding gives the session a concrete anchor. Leadership can agree who owns the response and how it is sequenced.

## 3. Four-Block Structure with Client-Specific Emphasis

The day runs as four 90-minute blocks with breaks. The working frameworks are:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

- **Output:** a shared statement of governance intent.
