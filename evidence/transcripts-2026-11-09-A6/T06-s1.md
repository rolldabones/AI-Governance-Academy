## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

In a consulting delivery, an external team writes the artifacts and hands them over. The Client receives documents its own people did not author and may not be able to operate.

In the Guided AI GRC Build, the Client's build team does the work. We provide:

- the method;
- weekly workshops and coaching;
- review of each artifact before the next stage begins.

At Week 12 the Client holds an AI Management System (AIMS) that its General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and internal auditors have built and can run.

This matters for Tamarack for three reasons:

1. **The audit finding needs an owner.** An inventory finding raised by internal audit is closed when the Client owns and maintains the inventory, not when a consultant produces one.
2. **The pilot needs Client ownership.** The visual inspection pilot will move through decisions after Week 12. The people making those decisions need to own the risk register and procedures that govern them.
3. **Certification readiness needs a running system.** ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work next year will require the Client to demonstrate a functioning system with operating evidence, which a handed-over document set does not provide.

**Frameworks.** The program uses two open frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Board-level material draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).

**Principles.** Three principles guide the work: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.
