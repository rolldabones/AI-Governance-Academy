## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A consulting engagement would hand Tamarack a finished set of documents. The Guided AI GRC Build works differently. The Client's own team builds the AI Management System (AIMS) week by week, with a live workshop, a coaching session and structured asynchronous work between sessions.

This matters for Tamarack for three reasons.

- **Familiar foundations.** The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function. The people who maintain those systems are the people best placed to extend them to AI.
- **Retained knowledge.** The knowledge needed to keep the AIMS running stays inside the Client, so the system does not depend on an outside party after Week 12.
- **Better starting point for ISO/IEC 42001.** A team that has built and operated the system is in a stronger position when that certification work starts. This is a readiness benefit, not a promise of certification.

The program uses two open frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Board-level material draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).

The program operates under three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.
