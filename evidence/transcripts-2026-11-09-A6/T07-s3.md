## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Maturity

A Client at the piloting stage faces three tasks at once:

- moving a pilot into production,
- operating a deployed system and
- preparing for a planned one.

Each task needs governance decisions at different moments. A single project would address one moment and leave the others unsupported. Continuous advisory keeps executive decisions, working-level implementation and readiness preparation connected across the year. This is particularly useful because the production target and the ISO/IEC 42001 start fall within the same planning horizon.

The engagement is grounded in three principles: **Slow AI**, **Informed Intent** and **Final Liability rests with the Human**. In practice this means:

- Decisions to move AI systems into production are taken deliberately.
- Decisions are taken with a clear understanding of purpose and risk.
- Accountable people at the Client own each decision.

The working frameworks are:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance structure, registers and evidence and
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate AI adoption practice.

Where executive or board reporting is needed, AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) serves as the board-layer reference.
