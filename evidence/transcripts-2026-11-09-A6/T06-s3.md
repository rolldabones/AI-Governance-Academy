## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Pure Consulting Delivery

In a conventional consulting engagement, a consultant writes the governance documents and hands them over. The Client receives artifacts but often lacks the people who know how to run them. The Guided AI GRC Build reverses that model:

- Your own build team produces every artifact during weekly workshops, coaching sessions and structured asynchronous work.
- Our role is to supply method, templates, challenge and review.
- At the end of twelve weeks, your team has built the system, retains it and can operate it without us.

This model fits Tamarack Instruments for three reasons:

1. **Incumbent systems.** The Client already runs an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function. Each new AI governance process should attach to these structures rather than sit beside them. That integration can only be done well by the people who run them.
2. **Lasting knowledge.** The two internal auditors on the build team gain working knowledge of the AIMS they will later review. The Client should decide how to preserve audit independence for any later assurance work.
3. **Relevant expertise.** Works council consultation and the EU data residency constraint call for local knowledge that our consultants do not hold.

The program applies three principles throughout: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

It draws on two working frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Board-layer material comes from AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
