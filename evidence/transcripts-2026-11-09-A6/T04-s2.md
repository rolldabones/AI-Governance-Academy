## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands, operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States, and has about 1,100 staff. The Client's AI portfolio is at three stages:

* **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
* **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
* **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The Client's AI maturity is piloting. The regulatory drivers named are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, and that start date is not fixed.

Three facts shape this proposal:

* An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that the Client has no inventory of AI systems.
* Dana Whitfield stated the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."
* Three constraints apply. Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year. Customer data must stay in the EU. The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## 2. Why Practitioner Capability Fits the Client Now

The Q2 2027 objective and the audit finding point to the same need: people inside the Client who can build and maintain governance artifacts themselves.

Over two days, the practitioner audience works directly in two repositories:

* GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
* Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

The audience roles are General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and two internal auditors. Participants leave with started artifacts and the knowledge of where each one lives.

The program also connects to two references:

* AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) at the board layer.
* The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) at the acceptance layer.

The Profile Block lists an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function. This proposal makes no assumption about the registers, processes, controls or records these hold. Participants may bring relevant documents to map against during the sessions.

The program is taught through three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

## 3. Running Worked Examples

Two use cases serve as the worked examples across both days.

* **AI visual inspection (piloting).** It is tied directly to the stated Q2 2027 objective. It also brings the works council constraint and the EU AI Act into the room.
* **Customer service chatbot (deployed).** It is the system already in use. It allows the customer data residency constraint and GDPR to be worked through in a governed workflow.

AI sales forecasting is used only in the inventory and classification session, so that the started inventory covers all three named systems.

**Data handling.** All exercise material is sanitized. Chatbot exercises use synthetic support interactions containing no customer data. Visual inspection exercises use a synthetic description of an inspection flow, and no exercise uses data about or involving production staff.
