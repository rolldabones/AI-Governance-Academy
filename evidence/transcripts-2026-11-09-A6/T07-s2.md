## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory

At Piloting maturity, governance has to keep pace with three use cases at different stages. It also has to keep pace with a production date and a management system standard whose preparation has yet to begin. A one-time assessment would capture the position at a single point in time. A fractional advisor provides continuity as each of these moves.

The engagement is guided by three principles:

* **Slow AI:** governance decisions are taken at a deliberate pace, ahead of deployment rather than after it.
* **Informed Intent:** each use case enters production with a documented understanding of its purpose, risks and controls.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** the Client's people own every decision. The advisor informs those decisions but does not make them.

The engagement works from two frameworks:

* The GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for registers, controls and evidence.
* The Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate use case intake and review.
