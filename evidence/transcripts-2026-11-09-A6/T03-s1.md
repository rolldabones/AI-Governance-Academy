# Executive Workshop Proposal

**Client:** Tamarack Instruments
**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel (Sponsor and stated budget holder)
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client's AI portfolio has three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding from June 2026 records that there is no inventory of AI systems. The Client names the EU AI Act and GDPR as regulatory drivers. It intends to begin ISO/IEC 42001 work next year, although the start date is not fixed. The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function.

The stated objective is: *"I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."* (Dana Whitfield)

The Client has stated three constraints:

- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

**AI maturity: Piloting.** The Client is at the point where governance choices can still shape how AI systems reach production rather than be retrofitted afterward. One system is live, one is in pilot and one is planned. The June 2026 audit finding shows the basic record of what AI the Client uses does not yet exist. A one-day session lets leadership agree on ownership, appetite and sequencing before the visual inspection pilot moves toward production. This avoids the slower path of agreeing those points system by system.

**Decision timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The Profile Block does not record the scope of that decision or who makes it, so both are `Unknown`. A one-day format produces a written decision log and draft roadmap in a single sitting. If the Workshop is scheduled ahead of 15 December 2026, that record is available as an input.

**Incumbent systems.** The Client already runs ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 9001 and internal audit structures. The Workshop therefore positions AI governance as an extension of those systems rather than as a parallel program.

## 3. Four-Block Structure with Client-Specific Emphasis

The Workshop is one day, live and virtual, structured as four 90-minute blocks with breaks. It draws on two frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

**Block 1, Alignment.** The room reviews the Client's current AI posture using the three use cases and the June 2026 audit finding. The emphasis is on what an AI inventory must contain and which systems fall within its first scope. *Output:* a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2, Principles.** The room agrees governance operating principles, including the three Academy principles applied to Tamarack's context:

- **Slow AI:** the path from visual inspection pilot to production.
- **Informed Intent:** what leadership needs to know about the deployed chatbot and the planned sales forecasting before endorsing their scope.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human:** named human accountability for AI-assisted outcomes.

The stated works council and EU data residency constraints are entered as principle inputs. *Output:* agreed principles ranked by the room.

**Block 3, Roadmap.** Governance work is sequenced against the EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001 and the incumbent frameworks. The Q2 2027 objective is the anchoring milestone. The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) is used to make "governance in place" testable as acceptance criteria. Whether the visual inspection deployment touches production staff is `Unknown`, so it is set as a checkpoint against the works council constraint. *Output:* a draft 12-month roadmap with owners.

The roadmap attaches to the incumbent systems as follows:

**Block 4, Decisions.** This is the executive decision session. The decision log covers four areas:

1. governance ownership

The block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* ([facilitation kit](https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md)) and the kit's twenty-minute closing exercise. This slot is the retention device for the three principles.
