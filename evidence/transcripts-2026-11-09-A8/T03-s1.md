# Executive Workshop Proposal: Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy
**Format:** One day, live virtual | **Price:** US$7,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States.

The Client's AI use cases are at three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) and GDPR (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj). The certification target is ISO/IEC 42001 (https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html), with work to start next year. That start date is not fixed.

The incumbent frameworks are:

- an ISO/IEC 27001 (https://www.iso.org/standard/82875.html) information security management system
- an ISO 9001 (https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html) quality management system
- an internal audit function

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded no inventory of AI systems.

The stated objective, in Dana Whitfield's words, is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

The stated constraints are:

- budget approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year
- customer data must stay in the EU
- the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

**AI maturity.** The Client's AI maturity is Piloting. At this stage the main governance choices can still be made before systems scale: ownership, risk appetite, oversight cadence and the order of work. A single executive day settles those choices so that later practitioner and implementation work has a mandate to follow.

**Decision timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The one-day format can be scheduled at short notice. We will confirm at intake what that decision concerns and whether the Workshop should take place before that date.

**Objective horizon.** The Q2 2027 production objective gives the roadmap a fixed reference point. The Workshop will work backwards from it. It will also define what "governance in place" means in acceptance terms, using The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done).

**Budget.** The Workshop price of US$7,500 sits within the stated approval of up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
