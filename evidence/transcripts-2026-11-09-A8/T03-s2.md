# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy
**Format:** 1 day, live virtual, four 90-minute blocks with breaks
**Price:** US$7,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States.

The Client's AI portfolio spans three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, but the start date is not fixed.

The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function.

The stated objective is direct: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." The stated constraints are:

- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

The Client's AI maturity is Piloting. At this stage the decisions that shape all later work sit with leadership: who owns AI governance, how much AI risk the Client will accept, how often oversight happens and what budget mandate supports it. A single day concentrates those decisions and records them before practitioner effort begins. This keeps later work from being built on assumptions that leadership has not confirmed.

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. The Workshop is a single day and needs light preparation, so it can be scheduled to fit that timeline. The date will be confirmed at intake.

The Workshop also gives the Client a roadmap sequenced against the Q2 2027 objective. At US$7,500 it sits within the approved budget and leaves room for follow-on work should the Client choose it.

## 3. Four-Block Structure with Client-Specific Emphasis

**Block 1: Alignment**
The session starts from the internal audit finding that there is no inventory of AI systems. Leadership will walk through the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting use case. The domains of the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) structure the conversation: governance structures, inventories, risk management, policies and evidence.
