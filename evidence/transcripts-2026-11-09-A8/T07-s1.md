## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Stage

At Piloting maturity, governance decisions arrive in sequence rather than all at once. The Client has one system already deployed, a second approaching a production decision and a third planned. Each needs governance at a different point in time.

A single project would address one moment. A fractional advisor stays alongside the governance team as each use case moves forward. The same advisor can also align the ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work with the visual inspection production decision, so the two efforts support each other rather than competing for the same people.

The program works from three principles:

* **Slow AI**
* **Informed Intent**
* **Final Liability rests with the Human**

In practice, every governance decision is made deliberately, with its purpose stated and with accountability held by named people at Tamarack Instruments. The advisor supports those decisions and does not make them.

The program draws on two frameworks:

* GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
* Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Where executive or board reporting is needed, it uses AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
