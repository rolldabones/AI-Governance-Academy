**Subject: Executive Briefing proposal for Tamarack Instruments**

Dana Whitfield,

**Situation**

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. A customer service chatbot is deployed on the support portal. AI visual inspection is being piloted on the German production line, and AI sales forecasting is planned for next year. Your stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." Customer data must stay in the EU, and the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year.

**What the Briefing covers for the Client**

The Briefing will map the EU AI Act and GDPR to each use case. Each point is an expectation to verify with counsel, not legal advice.

* **Chatbot:** transparency toward users interacting with AI and handling of customer data within the EU.
* **Visual inspection:** risk classification, human oversight and the works council step.
* **Forecasting:** the governance questions to settle before build.

We will test readiness against the Client's current piloting maturity. We will then outline an operating model drawing on the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). Board reporting will be referenced to AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).

Three principles run throughout: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. ISO/IEC 42001 will be discussed as preparation only. The Briefing does not promise certification or audit outcomes.
