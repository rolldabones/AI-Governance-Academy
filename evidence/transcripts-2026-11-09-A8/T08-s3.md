## Situation summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI use cases are at three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded no inventory of AI systems. The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year, and its start date is not fixed.

Dana Whitfield, General Counsel and stated budget holder, has set the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

One Workflow, Thirty Days takes one priority workflow through the twelve steps of the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) under the Consultant Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen/blob/main/consultant-workbook.md). It measures that workflow against a matched baseline and ends on Day 30 with a sponsor decision on evidence: expand the specified scope, revise or stop. The engagement covers one workflow. It does not produce an inventory of AI systems.

## The workflow proposed

We propose **AI visual inspection on the German production line** as the engagement workflow.

**Fit to AI maturity.** The Client's AI maturity is Piloting, and visual inspection is the use case at the pilot stage. A pilot gives the engagement a bounded workflow in which supervised AI cases can be compared against a matched baseline before any production decision.

**Fit to the decision timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. Whether the Day 30 decision is to precede that date, inform it or stand apart from it will be confirmed at intake.

1. volume
