## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Stage

At the piloting stage, governance decisions recur rather than conclude. Each pilot that moves toward production raises new questions about risk classification, human oversight, data handling and evidence. A one-time assessment captures a single moment. A fractional advisor stays with the governance team as each decision arrives.

Three features of the Client's position make continuous support relevant:

* A production date of Q2 2027 is stated for visual inspection.
* An ISO/IEC 42001 effort is expected next year.
* A third use case, sales forecasting, is planned.

The program is designed to sequence these demands so that each one is prepared for before it becomes urgent.

The engagement is guided by three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. The program does the following:

* It uses the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance, risk and compliance artifacts.
* It uses the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate, staged adoption practice.
* It draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) for board-layer reporting.
