# Guided AI GRC Build: Proposal for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Guided AI GRC Build, twelve weeks, US$60,000

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. The company has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI maturity is at the piloting stage.

The Client has three AI use cases at different stages:

- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. The Client targets ISO/IEC 42001, with work expected to start next year and no fixed start date. An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function.

The stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." Three constraints apply:

- Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement would produce a governance system on the Client's behalf and leave it with the Client at the end. The Guided AI GRC Build works the other way. The Client's own build team drafts, adopts and runs each artifact during the program, supported by weekly workshops and coaching. At Week 12 the people who will operate the AI Management System (AIMS) are the people who built it. They understand why each control exists and can maintain the system without depending on outside authors.

This matters for Tamarack Instruments for three reasons:

1. **Existing management systems.** The Client already runs an ISMS, a quality management system and an internal audit function. An AIMS built inside the organization can be designed to sit alongside those systems rather than beside them as a separate structure.
2. **Evidence in the Client's own records.** Any future ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work will rely on evidence that the system operates in practice. A guided build starts that evidence in the Client's own records from Week 8.
3. **Human accountability.** The program is grounded in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. A team that builds its own governance holds the accountability that the third principle requires.

The program uses two open frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Steering outputs can draw on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) where board-level reporting is needed.
