# Practitioner Academy Proposal

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy
**Format:** 2 days, live virtual, four 90-minute working sessions per day
**Price:** US$12,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands, operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States and has about 1,100 staff. The Client's AI maturity stage is piloting. Its AI use cases are:

* A customer service chatbot deployed on the support portal.
* AI visual inspection in pilot on the German production line.
* AI sales forecasting planned for next year.

The EU AI Act and GDPR are the regulatory drivers. In June 2026 an internal audit finding recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year, though the start date is not fixed. The Client's incumbent frameworks are ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001, and it has an internal audit function.

Three constraints apply:

* Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

Dana Whitfield has stated: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

## 2. Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The Client has one deployed system, one in pilot and one planned. This gives participants three live reference points across the lifecycle, so the program can work on real use cases rather than hypothetical ones. The program will build the inventory and first governance artifacts in the room, with practitioners doing the work. This responds directly to the June 2026 audit finding. It also gives the team working material that it can carry into the ISO/IEC 42001 work and the Q2 2027 objective. The program does not promise any audit, certification or regulatory outcome.

**Worked examples selected.** Two use cases run through both days. The third appears once.

* **Customer service chatbot.** It is the only deployed system, which makes it the anchor for inventory, transparency and data-residency reasoning.
* **AI visual inspection pilot.** It is tied to the stated Q2 2027 objective and raises the oversight and works council questions.
* **Sales forecasting.** It is used once, in Session 2, to show how a planned system enters the inventory before build.

**Doctrine.** The program is grounded in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human.
