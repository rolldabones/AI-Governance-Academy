## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has its headquarters in the Netherlands, operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States and has about 1,100 staff. Its AI portfolio spans three stages:

* **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
* **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
* **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. Tamarack Instruments operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, an ISO 9001 quality management system and an internal audit function. In June 2026, an internal audit finding noted no inventory of AI systems. ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year, with the start date not yet fixed.

Dana Whitfield has stated the objective directly: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

Three constraints shape the work:

* Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
* Customer data must stay in the EU.
* The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

## Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits

The Implementation Accelerator establishes the foundation of an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) in five consecutive working days. That foundation covers the governance charter, AI system inventory, risk register, core policies and evidence model, built by the Client's own team in live sessions.

**The Q2 2027 objective.** The Accelerator produces the core governance artifacts in a single week. Most of the time before Q2 2027 then remains available for review, adoption and operation. The week does not depend on the visual inspection pilot reaching production.

**The ISO/IEC 42001 target.** ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year, and the start date is not fixed. The Accelerator structures every artifact for future conformity work. The intent is readiness: the program does not promise certification, audit results or any assessment outcome.

**The internal audit finding.** The June 2026 finding concerned the absence of an AI system inventory. Day 2 is dedicated to building that inventory and its classification scheme, with the three known use cases as the first entries.

**The budget.** At US$25,000, the Accelerator sits within the approved budget of up to US$80,000. Whether the engagement would fall within this fiscal year is a question to confirm at intake.

**The incumbent frameworks.** The program designs each artifact to attach to the ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, the ISO 9001 quality management system and the internal audit function rather than stand alone.
