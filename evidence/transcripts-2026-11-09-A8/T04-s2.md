# Practitioner Academy Proposal for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy
**Format:** 2 days, live virtual, four 90-minute working sessions per day
**Investment:** US$12,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States, with about 1,100 staff. Its AI portfolio has three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The Client describes its AI maturity as piloting. Its regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is planned to start next year, and the start date is not yet fixed. The Client's incumbent frameworks are ISO/IEC 27001, ISO 9001 and an internal audit function.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that no inventory of AI systems exists. Dana Whitfield has stated the objective directly: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

## 2. Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The Client has one system in service, one in pilot and one planned. That spread gives practitioners real material to govern without retrofitting a large estate.

The Q2 2027 production milestone sets a concrete horizon. The June 2026 finding identifies inventory as the first gap to work on.

The Practitioner Academy builds this capability inside the organization. Participants from Legal, Information Security, Quality, Data, plant operations and Internal Audit work directly in two repositories:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Participants leave with started artifacts and know where each one lives.

The program is anchored in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. The program does not deliver or promise ISO/IEC 42001 certification, EU AI Act conformity or the closure of any audit finding. It builds the practitioner capability and starting artifacts that later work can rely on.

## 3. Running Worked Examples

All three of the Client's use cases serve as working material, each in a distinct role:

1. **AI visual inspection (primary).** This use case is tied to the Q2 2027 objective and runs through every session.
2. **Customer service chatbot (secondary).** As the deployed system, it is the natural subject for governed prompting and the second governed workflow.
3. **AI sales forecasting (tertiary).** As a planned system, it shows how governance enters at intake. It appears in inventory, classification and risk assessment only.

**Confidentiality and constraints.** Two of the Client's constraints shape the exercise design:

- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

To respect both, all exercises use sanitized analogs and no real Client data enters the workshop:

- synthetic support transcripts for the chatbot
- written defect scenarios in place of production images or staff data for visual inspection
- a synthetic order history for forecasting

The visual inspection workflow will carry works council consultation as an explicit gate condition.
