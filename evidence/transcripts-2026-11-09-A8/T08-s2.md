# One Workflow, Thirty Days

**Proposal to Tamarack Instruments**
**Prepared for Dana Whitfield, General Counsel**

## 1. Situation

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI use is as follows:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

Its AI maturity is Piloting. An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year, and the start date is not fixed.

The Client's stated constraints are:

- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

Dana Whitfield has stated the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

One Workflow, Thirty Days takes one priority workflow through the twelve steps of the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) under the working records of the Consultant Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen/blob/main/consultant-workbook.md). It measures the workflow against a matched baseline. It ends on Day 30 with a sponsor decision on evidence.

This engagement covers one workflow. It does not produce an inventory of AI systems.

## 2. The Workflow Proposed

We propose the **AI visual inspection workflow on the German production line** as the leading candidate.

- **Fit with the stated objective.** It is the workflow named in the stated objective.
- **Fit with the Client's AI maturity.** It is piloting, which matches the Client's AI maturity of Piloting. The engagement's bounded rehearsal and supervised cases are designed for a workflow at that stage.
- **Alternate candidate.** The customer service chatbot is proposed as the alternate.
- **Planned use case.** Sales forecasting is planned for next year and will be scored alongside the others.

The final selection is made in W02 against the benefit scale. The scale scores four factors from interview and work-sample evidence: volume, active human effort per unit, rework and queue burden and consequence of better or faster output. If W02 favors another workflow, the engagement follows W02.

On timing, a decision is expected by 15 December 2026. How the thirty-day calendar is placed relative to that date is a question to confirm at intake.

**Works council precondition.** Because the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff, the engagement will not interview production staff or run supervised cases with them until the Client confirms the consultation position. Whether consultation is required for this engagement's interviews and cases is a question to confirm at intake with counsel.
