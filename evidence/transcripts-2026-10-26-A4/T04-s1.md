# Practitioner Academy Proposal for Tamarack Instruments

## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has three AI use cases at different stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

Three facts shape the Client's position:

1. **An open audit finding.** An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that no inventory of AI systems exists.
2. **Regulatory drivers.** The EU AI Act (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) and GDPR (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) apply.
3. **A future certification target.** ISO/IEC 42001 (https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html) is a target, with work expected to start next year on a date not yet fixed.

Your stated objective is direct: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

## Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

**The gap is at the working level.** The audit finding is about inventory. Closing that gap requires people who can build and maintain the artifacts themselves, not a policy statement from outside. The Practitioner Academy has participants start that work inside the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). They leave with drafts they own and know where each one lives.

**The timing gives a usable window.** The visual inspection pilot has not yet reached production. Governance artifacts can therefore be drafted while design choices remain open. This is easier than retrofitting them after rollout.

**The program respects your constraints.**

- **EU data residency.** Customer data must stay in the EU, so the program uses no real customer data.
- **Works council consultation.** The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff. Session artifacts are drafts for the Client's own review and are not put into operation by the program.

**Existing management systems are a starting point to verify.** The Client lists an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS (https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) and an ISO 9001 quality management system (https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html) as incumbent frameworks. Whether specific registers, procedures or records from those systems can be reused is `Unknown` and will be confirmed with participants during the program.

**The doctrine comes first.** The program is anchored in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

## Worked Examples

Three use cases run as worked examples across both days, each chosen for a different reason:

1. **AI visual inspection is the primary example.** It is tied directly to your Q2 2027 objective and sits closest to the works council constraint.
2. **The customer service chatbot is the second example.** It is already deployed and must be inventoried first. It also tests the EU data residency constraint.
3. **AI sales forecasting is used lightly, as the intake case.** Because it is still planned, it lets participants practice governing a system before it is built.

**Data handling for the examples.** All exercises use sanitized analogs:

- Synthetic support conversations stand in for real chatbot interactions.
- Process descriptions and generic defect categories stand in for production line data.
- No data identifying customers or production staff is used.
