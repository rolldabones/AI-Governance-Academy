## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. The Client is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its stated AI maturity is Piloting, with three AI use cases at different stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

In June 2026, an internal audit finding recorded that the Client holds no inventory of AI systems. The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, although the start date is not fixed. Your objective is plain: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

## 2. Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The Client has one system in deployment and one in pilot, with a fixed production target. Governance needs to be built by the people who will run it, working on real systems rather than abstractions.

The Practitioner Academy places seven roles in the same working sessions for two days:
- General Counsel
- CISO
- Head of Quality
- Head of Data
- German plant manager
- Two internal auditors

These participants build first versions of the artifacts the audit finding and the Q2 2027 objective call for. The program is a starting point for capability. It does not by itself close the audit finding, and it does not promise any certification or audit outcome.

The Client holds an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and an ISO 9001 quality management system. Which existing procedures, records or controls under those systems can be reused for AI governance is Unknown. This proposal does not assume any. Participants will identify candidate connection points for the Client to confirm.

The program teaches three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human.
