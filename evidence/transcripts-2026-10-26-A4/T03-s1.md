# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**To:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel (stated as budget holder)
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy
**Source:** Client Profile Block v1.0 and discovery call notes of 6 October 2026 (Dana Whitfield, Ravi Okafor and Ingrid Solberg)

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client's AI portfolio is at three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems.

Your stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

**Drivers and constraints**

- **Regulatory drivers:** the EU AI Act and GDPR.
- **Certification target:** ISO/IEC 42001 ([iso.org/standard/42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001)). Work is to start next year and the start date is not fixed.
- **Customer data:** must stay in the EU.
- **German works council:** must be consulted before anything touches production staff.
- **Budget:** approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

**Incumbent frameworks.** The Client operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS ([iso.org/standard/27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001)) and an ISO 9001 quality management system ([iso.org/standard/62085.html](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html)). We propose to position AI governance as an extension of both rather than as a parallel structure.

The Profile Block does not identify the registers, processes, controls or records held under either system. The specific attachment points for AI governance are therefore `Unknown` until the Client confirms them.

**Points the Profile Block does not settle**

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Whether the chatbot processes personal data or customer data | `Unknown` |
| Where the chatbot processes or stores data | `Unknown` |
| Whether visual inspection touches production staff, and so whether works council consultation applies to it | `Unknown` |
| The EU AI Act classification of any of the three use cases | `Unknown`. Requires confirmation with external counsel. |

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits Now

**AI maturity.** The Client is at the Piloting stage, with one system deployed, one in pilot and one planned. At this stage the governance decisions are still inexpensive to make and to change. The workshop sets ownership, risk appetite and oversight cadence before a second system reaches production.

**Decision timeline.** The Profile Block records a decision expected by 15 December 2026. The subject of that decision is not stated and is `Unknown`. We recommend scheduling the workshop so that its decision log and draft roadmap are available before that date.

**Time to the objective.** Q2 2027 leaves a short window. A single day allows leadership to agree what "governance in place" means for visual inspection and to sequence the work required to reach it.

**Budget.** The fee is US$7,500. This sits within the stated US$80,000 approval and leaves the remainder available for whichever follow-on work the workshop record supports.

## 3. Workshop Structure

The workshop runs as four 90-minute blocks with breaks, delivered live and virtual. It draws on two Academy frameworks:

- The GRC Workbook ([github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook](https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook))
- The Slow AI Kitchen ([github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen](https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen))

**Block 1: Alignment**
- Map the current AI posture across the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and planned sales forecasting.
- Use the June 2026 internal audit finding on the missing AI inventory as the anchor.
- Output: a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2: Principles**
- Apply the three Academy principles to the Client's context:
  - **Slow AI:** pacing the move from pilot to production.
  - **Informed Intent:** what leadership must know before approving a use case.
  - **Final Liability rests with the Human:** named human accountability for each AI-assisted decision on the production line and in customer service.
- Output: agreed principles ranked by the room.

**Block 3: Roadmap**
- Sequence governance work against the EU AI Act, GDPR and ISO/IEC 42001.
- Extend the ISMS and the quality management system rather than duplicate them.
- Build in the EU data residency constraint and the works council consultation requirement.
- Plan backwards from Q2 2027, using The Definition of Done ([github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done](https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done)) to frame acceptance criteria for "governance in place."
- Output: a draft 12-month roadmap with owners.

**Block 4: Decisions**
- Take executive decisions on governance ownership, risk appetite for AI, oversight cadence and budget mandate.
- Draw on AI Governance for Boards ([github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards](https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards)) for board-level reporting.
- Close with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and the twenty-minute closing exercise from the [facilitation kit](https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md). This is the retention device for the three principles.

## 4. Deliverables

1. **Decision log.** Records decisions taken in Block 4, with owners and review dates. Items involving the EU AI Act or GDPR are flagged for confirmation with external counsel.
2. **Prioritized roadmap.** A draft 12-month plan with owners assigned by the room, sequenced toward the Q2 2027 objective and the ISO/IEC 42001 start.
3. **Readiness observations.** Our observations on governance posture across governance structures, inventories, risk management, policies and evidence, drawn from the GRC Workbook domains.

**What the workshop does not provide**
- Regulatory content is orientation, not legal advice.
- The workshop does not promise any certification or audit outcome, including for ISO/IEC 42001.
