## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement would hand the Client a finished set of documents. The Guided AI GRC Build takes a different approach. Your own team builds the system each week with structured workshops, coaching and asynchronous work between sessions. When the program ends, the people who will operate the AIMS are the people who wrote it.

This approach suits Tamarack for three reasons:

1. **The inventory finding concerns ownership as much as documentation.** A team that has built and populated the inventory itself is positioned to keep it current.
2. **The visual inspection deadline continues past Week 12.** The Client will need internal capability to apply the governance to production deployment in Q2 2027 without external dependency.
3. **The future ISO/IEC 42001 effort depends on internal competence.** Readiness for that effort rests on whether the Client's people can explain and run their own management system.

The program works from three open frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)
- AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards), used for the steering layer

It is guided by three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.
