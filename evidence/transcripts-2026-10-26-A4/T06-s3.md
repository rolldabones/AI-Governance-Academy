## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A consultant-delivered AIMS typically arrives as a finished document set that the Client must then learn to operate. The Guided AI GRC Build reverses that sequence. Your build team produces every artifact during the program, with weekly workshops to teach the method, coaching to resolve Client-specific questions and structured asynchronous work to do the building.

At Week 12, the people who will run the system are the people who built it. That matters for Tamarack Instruments for three reasons:

1. **The internal audit finding requires an operating process, not a one-time list.** The inventory must stay current as visual inspection moves toward production and sales forecasting enters planning.
2. **The AIMS must work inside existing systems.** It needs to sit inside the Client's existing ISMS and QMS rather than beside them, and only your own team knows those systems well enough to integrate rather than duplicate.
3. **Retained capability supports later ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work.** It does not depend on external parties returning to explain their own deliverables.

The program is guided by three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. The working frameworks are:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

For steering-level material, we draw on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
