## 2. Why Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Stage

At the piloting stage, governance decisions are made alongside delivery decisions rather than after them. The visual inspection pilot has a production target. The chatbot is already live. Sales forecasting is planned. ISO/IEC 42001 work follows.

A single assessment would describe the position at one point in time. It would not support the sequence of decisions between now and production, or between production and certification readiness. A fractional advisor provides continuity across that sequence without adding headcount.

The engagement applies three principles throughout:

* **Slow AI:** deliberate pacing of deployment against governance readiness.
* **Informed Intent:** decisions made with stated purpose and known risks.
* **Final Liability rests with the Human:** accountability for AI outcomes remains with named people at the Client.
