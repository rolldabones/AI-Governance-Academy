## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement typically produces a governance system for the Client and then hands it over. A guided build reverses this. Your team builds the Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) in weekly workshops, refines it in coaching sessions and puts it to work through structured asynchronous assignments. At Week 12, the people who must operate the system are the people who designed it.

Three reasons favor this approach for Tamarack Instruments.

**Integration rather than duplication.** The Client lists an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and an ISO 9001 quality management system as incumbent frameworks. A guided build lets your team decide, week by week, how each AIMS artifact attaches to what already exists. This avoids a parallel structure. The Profile Block does not identify the specific registers, procedures or records within those systems. Each integration point is therefore recorded as `Unknown` below and will be confirmed with your team in Week 1.

**Retained capability.** The contracted outcome is a system your team can operate after the program ends. It is not a document set that depends on outside authors.

**Pace that matches the decision.** The program follows three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice, visual inspection moves to production only when your team can show the governance around it. The calendar does not set that pace.

The program draws on three open frameworks:
