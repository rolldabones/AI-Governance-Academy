## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at Piloting Maturity

At Piloting maturity, governance decisions are being made in real time. The Client has a deployed system, a pilot approaching production and a planned use case. Each sits at a different point in its lifecycle, and the Client has a fixed production target and a certification ambition with an open start date.

A one-time assessment would capture the position at a single moment. A fractional advisor stays with the executive team as the visual inspection pilot moves toward production. The advisor helps sequence the inventory, decision records and oversight design so they are ready when needed and are not reconstructed afterward.

The engagement applies three principles throughout: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. Every recommendation is advisory. Decisions and accountability remain with the Client's executives.
