# Proposal: Practitioner Academy for Tamarack Instruments (fictional)

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy
**Basis:** Client Profile and discovery call notes of 6 October 2026

## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has one deployed AI system (a customer service chatbot on the support portal), one in pilot (AI visual inspection on the German production line) and one planned for next year (AI sales forecasting). An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that no inventory of AI systems exists. The stated objective is clear: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

The EU AI Act and GDPR are the named regulatory drivers. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, although the start date is not fixed. The Client names ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 as incumbent frameworks. This proposal does not assume that any specific register, control or procedure exists under them. Where the program connects to them, the current state is `Unknown` until the Client confirms it.

## Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

The audit finding and the Q2 2027 production date both call for working artifacts rather than awareness training. Over two days, the Practitioner Academy has the Client's General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and two internal auditors build the first governance artifacts together inside the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). Participants leave knowing where each artifact lives and how to continue the work without outside support. The program rests on three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human.

## Running Worked Examples

- **Visual inspection pilot (primary).** This system is tied directly to the stated objective. It sits where quality, EU AI Act classification and the works council constraint meet.
- **Customer service chatbot (secondary).** This is the only deployed system. It tests the GDPR and EU data residency constraints and the design of escalation to a human.
- **Sales forecasting** enters the program only as a planned inventory entry, so that the inventory reflects all three systems.

**Confidentiality approach.** No customer data enters the exercises or the repositories. Chatbot exercises use synthetic support transcripts. Visual inspection exercises use synthetic defect descriptions and contain no information about production staff. All artifacts are drafts. The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff. Whether that consultation has begun is `Unknown`.
