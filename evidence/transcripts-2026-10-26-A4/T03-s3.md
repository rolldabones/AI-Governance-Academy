# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel and Sponsor
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client's AI activity spans three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

The Client describes its AI maturity as Piloting. In June 2026 an internal audit finding recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems. The regulatory drivers named are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, although the start date is not fixed.

The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system and an ISO 9001 quality management system. Its stated constraints are:

- A budget approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

Your stated objective is clear: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits Now

**Maturity.** At the Piloting stage the decisions that shape governance have not yet been locked in by production use. One of the three use cases is already deployed, one is in pilot and one is planned. That spread lets the leadership group set ownership, risk appetite and oversight once and apply them across all three stages, rather than retrofitting controls later.

**Timeline.** A decision by 15 December 2026 leaves a short window before the Q2 2027 production target for visual inspection. A single structured day produces the decisions and draft roadmap that the following months of work depend on. It does not require a long engagement before anything is agreed.

**Existing systems.** The Client holds an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and an ISO 9001 QMS. The Workshop positions AI governance as an extension of these systems rather than a parallel structure. The Profile Block does not state which registers, processes, controls or records within them would host AI governance. Specific attachment points are therefore `Unknown` and will be identified with the room.
