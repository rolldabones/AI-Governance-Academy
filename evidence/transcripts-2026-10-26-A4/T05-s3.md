## 2. Why an Intensive AIMS Foundation Build Fits Now

**Timing.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026, and visual inspection is targeted for production in Q2 2027. A five-day intensive held early in 2027, on dates to be agreed, would give the Client a working governance foundation with the remaining months before Q2 2027 available for review, adoption and the works council consultation. A longer engagement starting at the same point would leave less of that window for adoption.

**Certification readiness, not certification.** ISO/IEC 42001 is a target for next year. The Accelerator structures every artifact for future conformity work: scope, roles, inventory, risk assessment, policies and evidence. The Accelerator does not promise certification or any audit result. Its purpose is readiness, so that the Client starts its ISO/IEC 42001 work from adopted documents rather than from a blank page.

**The audit finding.** Day 2 produces the AI system inventory that the June 2026 finding identified as absent. Whether that inventory closes the finding is a matter for internal audit to determine.

**Attachment to existing systems.** The Client's ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 management systems are the natural home for AI governance. Each artifact will be designed to attach to them rather than stand alone. **Principles.** The work follows three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice this means each AI system has a named human accountable for its outcomes, and each deployment decision is recorded with its intended purpose.

## 3. The Five-Day Build Spine, Tailored to Tamarack Instruments

The sequence is deliberate. The charter comes before the policies, so that each policy has an owner with decision rights to adopt it. The inventory comes before the risk register, so that each risk is tied to a known system.
