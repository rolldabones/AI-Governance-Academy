The leadership questions come before the build questions: who owns AI governance, how much AI risk the Client will accept and what "governance in place" must mean before the visual inspection system enters production. **Budget.** At US$7,500, the Workshop uses a small share of the approved US$80,000. The balance remains available for whatever the leadership team decides to fund next. The fiscal year end date is Unknown.
