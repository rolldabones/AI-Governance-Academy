## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A consultant-delivered AIMS tends to arrive as a finished set of documents that the Client must then learn to operate. The Guided AI GRC Build reverses that sequence. Your own team builds each component in weekly workshops, refines it in coaching and puts it to work between sessions. By Week 12 the people who will run the system are the people who built it. They understand why each control exists and can maintain it after the program ends.

This matters for Tamarack Instruments for three reasons:

- **Ownership of the audit response.** The internal audit finding calls for an inventory the Client can keep current, not a snapshot prepared by outsiders.
- **Integration with existing systems.** Your ISMS and QMS owners are in the room. Integration decisions are therefore made by the people who hold those systems.
- **Operation in production.** Visual inspection governance has to work on a German production line with works council involvement. That requires the plant manager and Head of Quality to shape the operating procedures directly.

The program is grounded in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice, every governed workflow built during the program names the accountable human decision maker and the point at which human oversight applies.

The program draws on two open frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for management system artifacts
- the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for governed workflow design

Board-level reporting can reference AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
