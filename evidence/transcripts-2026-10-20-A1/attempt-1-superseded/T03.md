# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments
**Prepared by:** AI Governance Academy
**Basis:** Discovery call notes of 6 October 2026

## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff and operations in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client already uses AI in three places. A customer service chatbot is live on the support portal. AI visual inspection is being piloted on the German production line. AI sales forecasting is planned for next year. Your stated objective is clear: governance must be in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027.

Two facts shape where to start. First, the internal audit finding of June 2026 recorded that Tamarack Instruments has no inventory of AI systems. Without an inventory there is no reliable base for classification, risk assessment or oversight. Second, the Client already runs an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system and an ISO 9001 quality management system. AI governance should extend these systems rather than sit beside them. The Workshop will treat both as the intended home for AI governance. Whether specific registers, procedures or records within them can carry AI content is currently `Unknown` and will be confirmed with your team rather than assumed.

The operating context adds three constraints that the governance design must respect. Customer data must stay in the EU, which bears directly on the chatbot and on future sales forecasting. The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff, which bears directly on visual inspection. Spending is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year. The EU AI Act and GDPR are the named regulatory drivers. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, with no fixed start date.

## Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits Now

Tamarack Instruments is at the piloting stage. The questions in front of leadership are therefore about direction, ownership and sequence rather than control design. A one-day session suits that stage. It brings together the people who must agree before detailed work can begin: General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, the German plant manager and two internal auditors. It gives them a shared record of what they decided.

The timing also fits. You expect to decide on this engagement by 15 December 2026. A workshop held soon after that decision leaves the first months of 2027 for implementation before the Q2 production date. It also means that ISO/IEC 42001 preparation, whenever it starts, begins from agreed ownership and an agreed roadmap.

## The Four-Block Structure

The Workshop runs as four 90-minute blocks with breaks. It draws on the [GRC Workbook](https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance structures, inventories, risk management, policies and evidence. It draws on [Slow AI Kitchen](https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for deliberate, staged adoption.

**Block 1, Alignment,** establishes the Client's current AI posture. The room will work through the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting system, using the June 2026 audit finding as the anchor. The block produces a shared statement of governance intent. Particular attention goes to the question of who owns the AI inventory and where it should live.

**Block 2, Principles,** sets the governance operating principles. These include the three Academy principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. Each is applied to the Client's own systems. For visual inspection, Final Liability rests with the Human raises a concrete question about who signs off on inspection outcomes that affect product release, and how that connects to existing ISO 9001 responsibilities. For the chatbot, Informed Intent raises questions about customer transparency and EU data residency. The room will rank the agreed principles.

**Block 3, Roadmap,** sequences governance work over 12 months against the EU AI Act, GDPR, the ISO/IEC 42001 target and the existing ISMS and QMS. The Q2 2027 production date for visual inspection is the fixed point. Works council consultation will be placed on the roadmap as a dependency rather than an afterthought. The block produces a draft 12-month roadmap with named owners.

**Block 4, Decisions,** is the executive decision-making session. It covers governance ownership, risk appetite for AI, oversight cadence and budget mandate within the approved fiscal envelope. [The Definition of Done](https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) supports agreement on what "governance in place" means before production. [AI Governance for Boards](https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) supports agreement on what should be reported upward. The block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and its twenty-minute closing exercise from the [facilitation kit](https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md). This exercise is the retention device for the three principles.

## Regulatory Orientation

Discussion of the EU AI Act and GDPR in the Workshop is orientation, not legal advice. Three items require confirmation with external counsel:

1. How the visual inspection system is classified under the EU AI Act.
2. Any transparency obligations that apply to the customer service chatbot.
3. The GDPR and works council implications of any system that processes data about production staff.

Obligations arising from US operations are `Unknown` at this stage and should also be confirmed with counsel.

## Deliverables

Within an agreed period after the session, Tamarack Instruments will receive three documents.

1. **A decision log.** It records each decision taken, the options considered, the named owner and a review date.
2. **A prioritized 12-month roadmap.** It reflects the room's agreed sequence and owners.
3. **Readiness observations.** These are factual notes on where the Client's current position stands against its Q2 2027 objective and its ISO/IEC 42001 intention. They will separate what was confirmed in the room from what remains `Unknown`.

The Workshop does not assess or promise any certification or audit outcome.
