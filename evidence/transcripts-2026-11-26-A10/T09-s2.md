**Situation summary**

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. Its AI use cases are at three stages: a customer service chatbot deployed on the support portal, AI visual inspection piloting on the German production line and AI sales forecasting planned for next year. Its AI maturity is piloting. Your stated objective is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." The Client's regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. ISO/IEC 42001 is a certification target, with work to start next year on a date not yet fixed. Customer data must stay in the EU, and the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

**What the Briefing covers for the Client**

The Briefing maps EU AI Act and GDPR expectations to each use case, including transparency for the chatbot and the risk classification of visual inspection. These are expectations to verify with counsel, not legal advice. United States requirements appear only as a general landscape item. We will assess readiness across the three use cases against the Q2 2027 objective and show how works council consultation and EU data residency shape sequencing. The operating model draws on the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). Board-layer material comes from AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards). Three principles anchor the session: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human. The third holds that every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene. The session is designed for the General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and two internal auditors.

**Logistics and price**

The Executive Briefing is a two-hour live virtual session priced at US$3,000. The date is to be agreed.

**What the Client receives afterward**

The Client receives a follow-up memo recording the decisions surfaced and our readiness observations. It also sets out three prioritized recommendations, each mapped to a next program option. The Briefing supports preparation for ISO/IEC 42001. It does not promise certification, audit outcomes or regulatory approval.
