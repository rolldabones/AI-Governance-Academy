# One Workflow, Thirty Days

**Proposal to Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments**

## 1. Situation summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. Its AI use is at three stages:

* a customer service chatbot is deployed on the support portal;
* AI visual inspection is being piloted on the German production line;
* AI sales forecasting is planned for next year.

AI maturity is at the piloting stage. The EU AI Act and GDPR are named regulatory drivers. Work toward ISO/IEC 42001 is to start next year, with the start date not fixed. An internal audit finding of June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems.

Three constraints apply:

* customer data must stay in the EU;
* the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff;
* a decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

You have stated the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

## 2. The workflow proposed

We propose AI visual inspection on the German production line as the engagement's one workflow.

**Why it fits.** It is the use case closest to your stated objective, and it sits at the pilot stage. That matches Tamarack Instruments' piloting maturity. A pilot can be taken through a bounded rehearsal and supervised cases without the engagement deciding a production release. Starting now leaves room after Day 30 for a revise outcome to be acted on before Q2 2027.

**Relation to the decision expected by 15 December 2026.** Whether the Day 30 decision is intended to inform that decision is a question to confirm at intake.

**Final selection.** The final selection is made in W02 against the benefit scale's four factors:

* volume;
* active human effort per unit;
* rework and queue burden;
* consequence of better or faster output.

The deployed chatbot is the alternative candidate if the scoring favours it.

**Regulatory note.** How the EU AI Act classifies visual inspection, and whether GDPR applies to the inspection data, are flagged for external counsel. This proposal gives orientation, not legal advice.
