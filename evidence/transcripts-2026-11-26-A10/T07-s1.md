## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at the Piloting Stage

At the Piloting stage, governance decisions are made while systems are moving toward production. They are not made after the fact. A one-time assessment would describe the position at a single point. It would not support the sequence of decisions between now and a Q2 2027 production target.

Fractional advisory provides steady executive support across that sequence. It covers:

- the inventory that addresses the internal audit finding
- authorization and ownership for each system
- a production readiness decision for visual inspection
- the start of ISO/IEC 42001 readiness work.

The program applies three principles throughout:

- **Slow AI** holds that AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- **Informed Intent** holds that no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human** holds that every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

The working frameworks are the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen). For any board-level reporting, the program draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
