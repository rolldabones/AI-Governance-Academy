## 3. Four-Block Structure with Client-Specific Emphasis

The session runs as four 90-minute blocks with breaks. It draws on two frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

**Block 1, Alignment.** The room reviews Tamarack Instruments' AI posture across the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting. The June 2026 internal audit finding is the starting point. The block produces a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2, Principles.** The room agrees and ranks governance operating principles, including the three Academy principles applied to each use case:

- **Slow AI:** AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- **Informed Intent:** no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human:** every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

Two stated constraints will be tested as principle inputs: the EU data residency requirement and works council consultation.

**Block 4, Decisions.** The room records decisions on four topics:
