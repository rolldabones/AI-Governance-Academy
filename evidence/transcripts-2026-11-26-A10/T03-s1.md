# Proposal: Executive Workshop for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Executive Workshop, AI Governance Academy
**Format:** One day, live virtual | **Price:** US$7,500

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States.

The Client's AI portfolio spans three stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded no inventory of AI systems.

**Regulatory and standards context:**

- The regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR.
- ISO/IEC 42001 work is to start next year. The start date is not fixed.

**Incumbent frameworks:**

- an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS
- an ISO 9001 quality management system
- an internal audit function

**Constraints:**

- Customer data must stay in the EU.
- The German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff.

Dana Whitfield has stated the objective: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027."

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

## 2. Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

The Client's AI maturity is Piloting. One system is deployed, one is in pilot and one is planned. At this stage, leadership can set ownership, appetite and oversight before a pilot reaches production, rather than retrofitting governance afterward.

A single day gives the audience roles a shared view of posture, agreed principles, a sequenced roadmap and recorded decisions. The full working program is not required to get there.

The session can be scheduled with the 15 December 2026 date in view. How the session relates to that date is a question for intake.

## 3. Four-Block Structure with Client-Specific Emphasis

The workshop draws on two frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

AI governance is positioned as an extension of the ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS, the ISO 9001 quality management system and the internal audit function, not as a parallel structure. Specific attachment points within these frameworks, such as registers, procedures or records, are `Unknown` and will be confirmed at intake.

**Block 1, Alignment (90 minutes).**
- Maps current AI posture across the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot and the planned sales forecasting.
- Uses the June 2026 internal audit finding as the anchoring risk event.
- Draws on the GRC Workbook's governance structures and inventory domains.
- *Output:* a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2, Principles (90 minutes).** The room agrees and ranks governance operating principles. These include the three Academy principles, applied to each use case:

- Slow AI: AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- Informed Intent: no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- Final Liability rests with the Human: every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

The session will test the visual inspection pilot against Slow AI ahead of any production decision.

**Block 3, Roadmap (90 minutes).**
- Sequences governance work against the EU AI Act, GDPR and the ISO/IEC 42001 target.
- Builds on the incumbent frameworks.
- Treats EU data residency and works council consultation as explicit sequencing gates.
- Uses The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) to help the room define acceptance criteria for "governance in place."
- *Output:* a draft 12-month roadmap with owners named by role.

**Block 4, Decisions (90 minutes).** An executive decision session covering four areas:

- governance ownership
- risk appetite for AI
- oversight cadence
- budget mandate

AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) informs how decisions are framed for board-level reporting.

The block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and the twenty-minute closing exercise from the facilitation kit (https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md). This closing slot is the retention device for the three principles.

## 4. Regulatory Orientation

Regulatory content in the workshop is orientation, not legal advice. The following items require external counsel:
