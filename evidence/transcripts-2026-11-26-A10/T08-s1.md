# One Workflow, Thirty Days

**Proposal to Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments**
AI Governance Academy, delivered under the Consultant Workbook of the Slow AI Kitchen

## 1. Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has three AI use cases:

* a customer service chatbot deployed on the support portal
* AI visual inspection in pilot on the German production line
* AI sales forecasting planned for next year

The Client's AI maturity is piloting. Its regulatory drivers are the EU AI Act and GDPR. Work toward ISO/IEC 42001 is to start next year, with the start date not yet fixed. An internal audit finding of June 2026 recorded that there is no inventory of AI systems.

The Client's stated objective, in Dana Whitfield's words, is: "I want governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027." The stated constraints are:

* customer data must stay in the EU
* the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff
* a budget approval for this fiscal year

A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

## 2. The Workflow Proposed

We propose AI visual inspection on the German production line as the engagement's one workflow.

**Why this workflow fits.**

* **Maturity.** It is the use case in pilot, which matches the Client's piloting maturity. A pilot can be taken through a bounded rehearsal and supervised cases without first building a system from nothing.
* **Objective.** It is the use case named in the stated objective. The Q2 2027 production date gives the Day 30 decision a clear purpose: evidence on whether the specified scope should expand, be revised or stop before production.
* **Timeline.** A decision is expected by 15 December 2026. What that decision concerns, and how it relates to this engagement, is a question to confirm at intake. The start date will be set against both dates once confirmed.

**Final selection.** The final selection is made in W02, against the Workbook's benefit scale, from interview and work-sample evidence. That evidence covers volume, active human effort per unit, rework and queue burden and the consequence of better or faster output. If W02 scores the customer service chatbot higher, the engagement proceeds on that workflow and the EU data residency constraint governs its evidence handling.

**Works council.** The Client has stated that the German works council is consulted before anything touches production staff. Discovery interviews and supervised cases on the production line would involve production staff. The timing and form of that consultation is a question to confirm at intake, and it may set the earliest practical Day 1.
