## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory

At the piloting stage, governance decisions arrive in sequence rather than all at once. Over the coming year these include:

- a pilot moving toward production;
- a deployed system that needs review against the data residency constraint;
- a planned system that needs authorization before it is built;
- a certification effort whose start date is still open.

A one-time assessment would capture one moment of this sequence. A fractional advisor stays with the Client as each decision comes due. Each decision is tested against the three principles of the program.

- **Slow AI** holds that AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- **Informed Intent** holds that no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human** holds that every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.
