## 2. Why Continuous Fractional Advisory at Piloting Maturity

At the Piloting stage, decisions about controls, ownership and evidence are being made in real time. Those decisions shape what a later ISO/IEC 42001 effort will need to prove. A one-off assessment captures a single moment. A fractional advisor stays with the Client through the visual inspection production decision, the planning of sales forecasting and the start of ISO/IEC 42001 work.

The engagement applies three principles:
- **Slow AI** holds that AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit.
- **Informed Intent** holds that no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions.
- **Final Liability rests with the Human** holds that every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene.

The working frameworks are:
- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

Executive-layer material draws on AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).
