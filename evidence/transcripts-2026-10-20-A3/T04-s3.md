The gap named by internal audit is practical: there is no inventory. The deadline you have set is also practical: governance must be in place before visual inspection reaches production. We have selected three of the Client's use cases as working material across both days.

1. **AI visual inspection (primary).** This is the system tied to your Q2 2027 objective. It also carries the works council dependency, so it is the clearest test of governance before production.
2. **Customer service chatbot (secondary).** It is the only deployed system. It exercises GDPR considerations and the EU data residency constraint.
3. **AI sales forecasting (inventory and classification only).** It shows how a planned system enters the inventory before it is built.
