**Audit finding.** The missing AI system inventory is closed directly on Day 2. The inventory is then used as the foundation for the Day 3 risk register.

**Certification target.** ISO/IEC 42001 work starts next year. **Incumbent systems.** Every artifact is designed to attach to the existing ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 systems rather than stand alone. This reduces duplication and keeps AI governance inside processes the Client already operates.
