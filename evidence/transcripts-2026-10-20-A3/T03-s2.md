## Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits Now

Tamarack Instruments is at the piloting stage. At this stage the most consequential governance choices are still open and still inexpensive to make. Those choices include who owns AI governance, how much AI risk the Client will accept and how oversight will run. Once visual inspection moves into production, the same choices become harder to change.

A single day lets the leadership group make those choices together. It does not commit the Client to a longer engagement before the direction is agreed. Your timeline records a decision expected by 15 December 2026. The workshop date is `Unknown` until agreed. If the session is held before that date, its decision log and draft roadmap are available to inform that decision.

The workshop also gives the June 2026 audit finding a clear executive response. It places that response within the frameworks the Client already runs, so it does not create a parallel structure.

## Workshop Structure and Emphasis for Tamarack Instruments

The day runs as four 90-minute blocks. It draws on the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen).

**Block 1, Alignment.** The group reviews the Client's current AI posture through the three use cases and the audit finding on the missing AI inventory. The aim is a shared view of what is deployed, what is in pilot and what is planned. The block produces a shared statement of governance intent anchored to the Q2 2027 production milestone for visual inspection.

**Block 2, Principles.** The group sets governance operating principles. These include the three Academy principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. Each is applied to the Client's context:

- Slow AI is tested against the gate between the visual inspection pilot and production.
- Informed Intent is tested against the chatbot already in service and the planned forecasting system.
- Final Liability rests with the Human is tested against the question of who accepts AI outputs and on what criteria. The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) serves as the acceptance-layer reference.

The room ranks the agreed principles.
