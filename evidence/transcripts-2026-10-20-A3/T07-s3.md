## 2. The Case for Continuous Fractional Advisory at Piloting Maturity

At Piloting maturity, governance decisions are made at the same pace as deployment decisions. One system is live. Another is moving toward production with a fixed internal date. A third is about to enter design. A single assessment would capture the position at one point in time. It would not travel with the Client as each system changes stage.

Fractional advisory provides a steady senior presence through that transition. The work proceeds under three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice this means production decisions are paced to evidence, each AI use is documented with a stated purpose before it scales and accountability stays with named Client decision makers. The advisor recommends. The Client decides.

The engagement draws on two open frameworks: the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and the Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen).
