# Proposal: Practitioner Academy for Tamarack Instruments

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy
**Basis:** Discovery call of 6 October 2026

## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments from its headquarters in the Netherlands, with operations in Germany and the United States. It is building AI capability in three stages:

* **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
* **Piloting:** AI visual inspection on the German production line.
* **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

Two pressures meet in 2027. First, you have set a clear objective: governance must be in place before visual inspection enters production in Q2 2027. Second, the June 2026 internal audit finding recorded that Tamarack has no inventory of AI systems.

The EU AI Act and GDPR frame the regulatory exposure. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to begin next year, though the start date is not yet fixed.

Tamarack already has two strong foundations: an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and an ISO 9001 quality management system. The task is not to build governance from nothing. It is to extend disciplines Tamarack already runs so they cover AI systems.

## Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

Tamarack's maturity is Piloting. At this stage, governance decisions made now are cheap to shape and expensive to retrofit later. Your objective points to a specific deadline, a specific system and a specific site. That favors working sessions that produce usable artifacts over awareness training.

The Practitioner Academy places your General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and two internal auditors in the same working sessions. Each participant works directly in the GRC Workbook and Slow AI Kitchen repositories, so the team leaves knowing where every artifact lives and how to continue without outside help.

The program is anchored in three principles: **Slow AI**, **Informed Intent** and **Final Liability rests with the Human**.

## Running Worked Examples

**Selection 1: AI visual inspection (German production line).** This is the primary example. It is the system named in your objective and it carries the Q2 2027 deadline. It also engages the Head of Quality and the plant manager directly and sits inside the ISO 9001 change discipline.

**Selection 2: Customer service chatbot.** This is the secondary example. It is the only system already in production and it handles customer interactions. That brings in GDPR, the EU data residency constraint and the CISO's ISMS controls. It also gives the team a contrast between governing a live system and governing a pilot.

**Inventory and classification only: AI sales forecasting.** This system is planned rather than built. It enters the inventory and classification exercise so it is captured before procurement or development begins. It is not carried through the full two days.
