## 2. Why Continuous Fractional Advisory at This Stage

At the Piloting stage of AI maturity, governance decisions are being made in real time, often by different functions. Quality, Security, Data, the German plant and Legal each hold part of the picture. A one-off assessment would describe the gap but would not help these functions make consistent decisions as the visual inspection pilot moves toward production and sales forecasting moves toward build.

A fractional advisor provides senior governance judgment on a steady rhythm. It keeps the Q2 2027 production milestone and later ISO/IEC 42001 readiness on one coordinated path, and it lets your existing internal auditors and management system owners carry the work forward.

The engagement is grounded in three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. In practice, this means production decisions about visual inspection stay with named Tamarack leaders, and every recommendation is documented so those leaders can decide with full information.
