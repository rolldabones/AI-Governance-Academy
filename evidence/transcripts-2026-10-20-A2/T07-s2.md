1. **A dated objective.** The Client has a stated production target for visual inspection in Q2 2027.
2. **A documented gap.** The internal audit finding on the AI system inventory needs a response.
3. **A pipeline of use cases.** A forecasting use case is planned, so new systems will need intake and review.

A standing advisor helps the executive team sequence these demands, keep artifacts current as pilots change and prepare for ISO/IEC 42001 work without overbuilding ahead of need.
