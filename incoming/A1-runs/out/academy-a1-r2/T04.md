# Practitioner Academy Proposal

**Prepared for:** Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments
**Basis:** Discovery call notes of 6 October 2026
**Program:** Practitioner Academy, AI Governance Academy

## 1. Situation

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States and has about 1,100 staff. Tamarack runs three AI use cases at different stages:

- **Deployed:** a customer service chatbot on the support portal.
- **Piloting:** an AI visual inspection system on the German production line.
- **Planned:** AI sales forecasting next year.

Your stated objective is clear: governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027. A June 2026 internal audit finding recorded that Tamarack holds no inventory of AI systems. The EU AI Act and GDPR are the named regulatory drivers. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, although the start date is not fixed. Decision on this program is expected by 15 December 2026.

## 2. Why Hands-On Practitioner Capability Fits Now

Tamarack's open finding and its production deadline both call for working artifacts, not awareness. An inventory, a classification rationale and a set of risk entries have to be built by the people who will own them. Those people are the General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and internal audit.

The Practitioner Academy puts those seven roles in the same working room for two days. They build those artifacts directly in the GRC Workbook and Slow AI Kitchen repositories, using Tamarack's own use cases as the material.

Timing matters for two reasons:

- **Production deadline.** Visual inspection is still a pilot. Governance designed now can shape the production release instead of being retrofitted after it.
- **Board and auditor engagement.** The internal auditors who raised the finding take part. They help define what evidence will satisfy them before the work is done, not after.

Three principles anchor the program: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.

## 3. Running Worked Examples

All three use cases are carried across both days. Each serves a distinct teaching purpose.

1. **Customer service chatbot (deployed).** It is live and interacts with customers, so it brings GDPR, EU data residency and transparency questions into every session. It also tests governance against a system already in operation.
2. **AI visual inspection (pilot).** This is the system tied to your Q2 2027 objective. It brings in product quality, the human review of automated accept and reject decisions and the requirement to consult the German works council before anything touches production staff.
3. **AI sales forecasting (planned).** This is the clean case for intake. Participants govern a system before it exists, which is the pattern Tamarack will repeat for every future use case.

## 4. Two-Day Curriculum Spine

The program runs live and virtual over two days. Each day has four 90-minute working sessions.

## 5. Working Within Tamarack's Constraints

**Customer data.** No real customer data enters the repositories or the virtual sessions. Chatbot exercises use sanitized analog transcripts written to resemble support queries without containing customer information. This keeps the program consistent with the requirement that customer data stays in the EU.

**Production staff and the works council.** Visual inspection exercises produce design drafts only. No exercise touches the production line, production staff or their data, and no imagery of identifiable staff is used. Any workflow element affecting production staff is marked as draft pending works council consultation, and the German plant manager owns that dependency.

**Existing management systems.** The discovery notes name ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 as incumbent frameworks. The program assumes no specific register, control or record from them. Participants are invited to bring any existing artifacts they hold, so Day 1 drafts can be connected to them.

These artifacts are structured so they can feed later ISO/IEC 42001 work. The program does not promise certification, audit closure or any regulatory outcome.
