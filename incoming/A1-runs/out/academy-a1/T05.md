## 2. Why an Intensive Foundation Build Fits Now

**Timeline.** A decision by 15 December 2026 allows the Accelerator to run in the first weeks of 2027. Its Day 5 output is a prioritized 90-day implementation plan. That plan can then run before the Q2 2027 production decision on visual inspection, not after it. The Accelerator does not make governance complete by itself. It gives the Client a working foundation and a sequenced plan with named owners, which can be executed in the time that remains.

**Audit finding.** The June 2026 finding is addressed directly. On Day 2 the team builds the AI system inventory and its classification scheme. That gives internal audit a concrete artifact it can review when it follows up on the finding.

**ISO/IEC 42001.** Every artifact is structured for future conformity work. When ISO/IEC 42001 work begins next year, the Client starts from owned, reviewed artifacts rather than a blank page. This is readiness work. It does not promise certification or any audit outcome.

**Incumbent systems.** The Client already runs ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 management systems. We will design each artifact to attach to those systems rather than stand alone. This avoids a parallel governance structure. Which ISMS and QMS records serve as attachment points is not stated in the discovery notes and is therefore Unknown. We will confirm them in scoping.

**Budget.** At US$25,000, the Accelerator sits within the US$80,000 approved for this fiscal year. Whether the engagement and invoice fall within the current fiscal year depends on the fiscal year end date, which is Unknown.

**Principles.** The work follows three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. For Tamarack, this means one thing in practice. The visual inspection go-live is a deliberate decision, taken by named people, on documented evidence.
