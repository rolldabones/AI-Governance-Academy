## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement typically delivers a finished document set. The Client then has to absorb, adopt and operate it after the consultants leave. The Guided AI GRC Build reverses that sequence.

Your standing build team produces every artifact during the program, using two open frameworks:

- GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

We provide structure, coaching and review. Three benefits follow for Tamarack Instruments:

1. **Ownership.** The people accountable for the system have built it and can explain it to auditors, the works council and the board.
2. **Retention.** Knowledge stays inside the Client when the program ends. The Client is not left with a binder it cannot maintain.
3. **Operability.** Processes are tested against your real systems during the twelve weeks. Evidence is therefore being generated before handover rather than promised afterward.

The program applies three principles throughout: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human.
