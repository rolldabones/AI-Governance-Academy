# Executive Workshop Proposal for Tamarack Instruments

Prepared for Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Sponsor

## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has a customer service chatbot deployed on its support portal. It is piloting AI visual inspection on the German production line and plans AI sales forecasting for next year. An internal audit finding in June 2026 recorded that the Client holds no inventory of AI systems.

Your stated objective is to have governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027. A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

The Client operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system and an ISO 9001 quality management system. It intends to begin ISO/IEC 42001 work next year, with a start date not yet fixed. The regulatory drivers recorded in our discovery call of 6 October 2026 are the EU AI Act and GDPR.

Three constraints shape this work:

- **Budget:** approval of up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.
- **Data residency:** customer data must stay in the EU.
- **Works council:** the German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff.

## Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

At piloting maturity, the Client has real systems to examine but has not yet fixed the governance choices that will carry them into production. Those choices are executive in nature: who owns AI governance, how much AI risk the Client will accept, how often leadership reviews it and what budget it will commit. A single structured day lets the people who hold those choices reach them together. They can do so ahead of the 15 December 2026 decision point and early enough to shape the path to Q2 2027.

The fee of US$7,500 sits within the approved budget. It leaves the remainder available for whatever follow-on work the workshop record supports. We do not presume what that work will be.

## Workshop Structure

The Executive Workshop runs as one live virtual day of four 90-minute blocks with breaks. It draws on two frameworks:

- the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook)
- Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen)

**Block 1, Alignment.** We establish the Client's current AI posture from the three stated use cases and the June 2026 audit finding. We use the inventory and governance structure domains of the GRC Workbook. Several facts are Unknown on the current record and will be raised as open questions rather than assumed:

- where the chatbot processes customer data
- whether the visual inspection pilot touches production staff
- how each system is currently controlled

The block produces a shared statement of governance intent.

**Block 2, Principles.** The room agrees governance operating principles, including the three Academy principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. We apply each to the Client's own context:

- **Slow AI**, to the pace and gating of the move from pilot to production, using Slow AI Kitchen
- **Informed Intent**, to sales forecasting while it is still at the planning stage
- **Final Liability rests with the Human**, to the question of which named role answers for the outputs of the visual inspection system

The block produces agreed principles ranked by the room.

**Block 3, Roadmap.** We sequence governance work against the EU AI Act, GDPR and the ISO/IEC 42001 target. AI governance is positioned as an extension of the existing ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and ISO 9001 QMS rather than a parallel structure. The current record does not establish which registers, procedures or records within those systems could host AI controls, so those attachment points are Unknown until the Client confirms them.

Regulatory content in this block is orientation, not legal advice. In particular, the classification of each use case under the EU AI Act and the GDPR position of each system are items requiring confirmation with external counsel. Works council consultation and EU data residency are treated as sequencing constraints. Acceptance criteria for the production gate draw on The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done).

The block produces a draft 12-month roadmap with owners.

**Block 4, Decisions.** Leadership records decisions on four topics:

- governance ownership
- risk appetite for AI
- oversight cadence
- budget mandate within the approved US$80,000

Where oversight reporting extends to a board, we reference AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards).

The day closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and its twenty-minute closing exercise, run as the retention device for the three principles. It draws on the facilitation kit (https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md).

These deliverables support the Client's governance and ISO/IEC 42001 preparation. They do not constitute or promise any certification or audit outcome.
