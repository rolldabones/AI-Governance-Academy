## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A consulting engagement typically hands over a finished set of documents. A guided build works differently. Your own team builds the management system during the twelve weeks with weekly coaching, so the people who will operate it understand why each element exists and can run it when the program ends.

This matters for Tamarack for three reasons:

1. **Audit response.** The internal audit finding concerns an operational gap, not a missing document. Closing it depends on an inventory that the Client keeps current.
2. **Future certification work.** ISO/IEC 42001 work planned for next year will rely on evidence produced by people inside the organization.
3. **Accountability.** Our principles of Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human place accountability with the Client's own decision makers rather than with an outside adviser.

The build uses two open frameworks:

- **GRC Workbook** (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) for governance artifacts.
- **Slow AI Kitchen** (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen) for governed workflows and human oversight.
