# One Workflow, Thirty Days

## Proposal to Dana Whitfield, General Counsel, Tamarack Instruments

### Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client's AI use is at three stages. A customer service chatbot is deployed on the support portal. AI visual inspection is being piloted on the German production line. AI sales forecasting is planned for next year.

Your stated objective is to have governance in place before visual inspection goes into production in Q2 2027. The Client names the EU AI Act and GDPR as regulatory drivers. It intends to begin ISO/IEC 42001 work next year, with the start date not yet fixed. It operates an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS and an ISO 9001 quality management system.

An internal audit finding from June 2026 recorded that the Client holds no inventory of AI systems. Three constraints frame the work. Customer data must stay in the EU. The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff. A decision is expected by 15 December 2026.

### The Workflow Proposed and Why It Fits

We propose AI visual inspection on the German production line as the one priority workflow. It fits a Client whose AI maturity is Piloting, because a pilot is the stage at which a bounded rehearsal and a matched baseline can be run before production commitments harden. It also bears directly on your Q2 2027 objective. The engagement would give you a sponsor decision on evidence about this workflow well ahead of that date, provided the engagement starts in time.

The final selection is not made in this proposal. It is made in working record W02 against the benefit scale of the Consultant Workbook. That scale scores each candidate on four factors: volume, active human effort per unit, rework and queue burden and the consequence of better or faster output. The chatbot is the other candidate available from your current use cases and would be scored alongside visual inspection. The sales forecasting use is planned and has no operating workflow to measure yet.

Two matters need settling before Day 1 if visual inspection is confirmed. Because the workflow involves the production line, your works council constraint applies. The status of that consultation is `Unknown` to us. The business owner, operators, reviewers and technical owner of the workflow are also `Unknown` and will be named in the W01 charter.
