## Situation Summary

Tamarack Instruments designs and manufactures laboratory measurement instruments. It has about 1,100 staff, is headquartered in the Netherlands and operates in the Netherlands, Germany and the United States. The Client has one deployed AI system, a customer service chatbot on the support portal. It is piloting AI visual inspection on the German production line and plans AI sales forecasting next year.

Customer data must stay in the EU. The German works council must be consulted before anything touches production staff. Budget is approved up to US$80,000 this fiscal year.

The Client already operates an ISO/IEC 27001 information security management system and an ISO 9001 quality management system. ISO/IEC 42001 work is expected to start next year, although that start date is not fixed.

## Why a One-Day Executive Session Fits

The Client's AI maturity is Piloting, so the portfolio is still small enough for leadership to see as a whole. One system is live, one is in pilot and one is planned. This is the stage at which ownership, risk appetite and oversight cadence can be set before the systems scale, rather than retrofitted afterwards.

The audit finding gives the session a concrete starting point. The Q2 2027 production target gives it a deadline. The Profile records a decision expected by 15 December 2026. The subject of that decision is Unknown, and we would confirm with you how the Workshop date should relate to it.

A single day lets the General Counsel, CISO, Head of Quality, Head of Data, German plant manager and two internal auditors reach shared decisions in one sitting. At US$7,500, the Workshop sits well within the approved budget and leaves room for whatever follow-on work the Client chooses.

## Structure and Client-Specific Emphasis

The Workshop runs as four 90-minute blocks with breaks. It draws on the GRC Workbook (https://github.com/rolldabones/grc-workbook) and Slow AI Kitchen (https://github.com/rolldabones/slow-ai-kitchen).

**Block 1, Alignment.** The room reviews the Client's current AI posture using the chatbot, the visual inspection pilot, the planned forecasting system and the June 2026 audit finding. The block produces a shared statement of governance intent. Facts not stated in the Profile will be recorded as Unknown and resolved in the room, not assumed. These include the systems' owners, the data each system processes and where the chatbot is hosted.

**Block 2, Principles.** Leadership agrees governance operating principles for the Client and ranks them. These include the three Academy principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent and Final Liability rests with the Human. Each principle is applied to the Client's situation, for example the gating of the visual inspection pilot before production and accountability for customer interactions handled by the chatbot.

**Block 3, Roadmap.** The room sequences governance work against the EU AI Act, GDPR and the ISO/IEC 42001 target. The roadmap positions AI governance as an extension of the existing ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 9001 systems rather than a parallel structure. Specific attachment points are currently Unknown because the Profile does not state which registers, procedures or controls those systems hold. Two examples are whether the ISMS maintains an asset inventory that an AI inventory could extend and whether the QMS has a change control process that could govern the move from pilot to production. Confirming these is a Block 3 task.

The works council consultation requirement and the EU data residency constraint are carried into the roadmap as dependencies. Whether the visual inspection system touches production staff is Unknown and is for the room to determine. The block produces a draft 12-month roadmap with owners.

**Block 4, Decisions.** Executives record decisions on governance ownership, risk appetite for AI, oversight cadence and budget mandate. The board-layer reference AI Governance for Boards (https://github.com/rolldabones/ai-governance-for-boards) informs oversight design. The acceptance-layer reference The Definition of Done (https://github.com/rolldabones/definition-of-done) informs the conditions a system should meet before production.

The block closes with *The Turtle and the Phoenix* and its twenty-minute closing exercise, which serves as the retention device for the three principles. Its facilitation kit is at https://github.com/rolldabones/AI-Governance-Academy/blob/main/the-turtle-and-the-phoenix.md.
