## 1. Situation Summary

* Customer data must stay in the EU.

## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Consulting Delivery

A conventional consulting engagement would hand Tamarack a finished set of documents. That would answer the audit finding on paper. It would leave the Client dependent on outside parties to run, update and evidence the system. The Guided AI GRC Build reverses this. The Client's own team builds each artifact in structured weekly steps, with coaching and review at each stage. At Week 12, the people who must operate the system are the people who built it.

This matters for Tamarack for four reasons.

1. **ISO/IEC 42001 readiness depends on ownership.** Readiness work requires the Client to demonstrate an operating management system, and an operating system needs internal owners.
2. **Remediation is credible when owned by management.** The June 2026 finding is best closed by management ownership rather than by a consultant's deliverable.
3. **Internal knowledge is essential.** Decisions on production line oversight, works council consultation and EU data residency rely on knowledge only the Client holds.
4. **Integration needs the people who run the incumbent systems.** Building on the ISMS and QMS requires the CISO and Head of Quality to design the connections themselves.

The program applies three principles throughout: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human.
