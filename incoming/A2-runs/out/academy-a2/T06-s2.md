## 2. Why a Guided Build Rather Than Pure Consulting Delivery

A consulting engagement can deliver a well-written set of governance documents. It cannot deliver a team that knows how to run them. Your objective requires governance that is operating, rather than documented, when visual inspection moves toward production.

In the Guided AI GRC Build, your team builds each artifact during weekly workshops and asynchronous work. Coaching sessions test and strengthen that work. This approach gives the Client three things:

1. **Ownership.** Your team understands why each control exists and can defend it.
2. **Retention.** The knowledge stays inside Tamarack Instruments after the program ends.
3. **Operating capability.** By Week 12, the Client runs its own reviews, assessments and evidence cycles.

The program is guided by three principles: Slow AI, Informed Intent, Final Liability rests with the Human. Each principle applies directly to visual inspection. Production decisions informed by AI need deliberate deployment, documented intent and a named human accountable for outcomes.
